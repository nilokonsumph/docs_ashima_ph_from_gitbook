# Manager portal

use Manager Portal to view your team and check their employee information, schedules, timesheets, attendance, and leave. depending on your assigned access, you can also add, edit, or delete supported records and file requests for employees.

start with [manage your team in Manager Portal](access-and-team-scope/start-here-manage-your-team-in-manager-portal.md) to understand who you can see, including additional reporting levels when your administrator enables them. use **To Do** when requests are assigned to you for approval.

## recommended order

1. [confirm your portal and team scope](access-and-team-scope/).
2. [review requests waiting for your decision](approvals/).
3. [review timesheets, attendance, and overtime](timesheets-attendance-and-overtime/).
4. [review or change team schedules](shifts-and-schedules/).
5. [review leave, CTO, and balances](leave-and-balances/).
6. [maintain assigned employee records](workforce-and-employee-records/) only when that administrative action is part of your role.

Manager portal access has two layers: which employees you can see and which actions you can perform for them. two managers in the same company can therefore see different employees, pages, or controls.
