# start here: manage your team in Manager portal

Manager Portal gives you a view of your team. use it to check employee information, schedules, timesheets, attendance, and leave in the areas your administrator has made available to you.

your team can include employees who report directly to you. if your administrator enables additional reporting levels, it can also include employees who report to those people, up to three levels in total. for example, a manager can see their supervisors and, when the additional level is enabled, the employees reporting to those supervisors. your administrator can also give you access to employees in selected departments.

depending on your access, you may be able to add, edit, or delete supported records, assign shifts, or file requests for an employee. viewing a record does not automatically give you permission to change it. approval work has its own assignment: **To Do** shows requests waiting for your decision when you are an approver.

two managers in the same company can therefore see different employees and different actions. see [understand which employees and actions you can access](understand-which-employees-and-actions-you-can-access.md) for reporting levels and examples of how access works.

## choose what you need to do

| what you need to do                       | where to go                                                                      | what to confirm before acting                                   | what confirms completion                                 |
| ----------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| find an employee                          | **Workforce → Employee Directory**                                               | the employee belongs to your assigned team scope                | the intended employee record opens                       |
| review personal or employment information | **Workforce → Personal Details** or **Employment Details**                       | you have a business reason to view or change the information    | the saved record displays the intended value             |
| review or change time-entry access        | employee record → **Time Entry**                                                 | the company policy and the employee's work arrangement          | the employee's clocking controls show the saved settings |
| review timesheets                         | **Hours → Timesheet Logs**                                                       | date, employee, assigned shift, and existing punches            | the detail matches the intended employee and workday     |
| correct a timesheet                       | **Hours → Timesheet Logs** → add or edit                                         | the shift has ended and payroll updates are still open          | the corrected punches and totals appear after save       |
| review attendance                         | **Hours → Attendance**, **Punctuality**, **Rest Day**, or **Timesheet Absences** | the page answers the question you are investigating             | the detail and related shift, leave, or timesheet agree  |
| file overtime for an employee             | **Hours → Overtime**                                                             | date, shift, reason, and company deadline                       | the application appears with its current status          |
| review or change schedules                | **Shifts → Overview**                                                            | the correct employee, dates, and replacement shift              | the employee calendar shows the new assignment           |
| review leave history or balance           | **Leaves → Leave History** or **Leave Balance**                                  | employee, leave type, dates, and transaction history            | the detail and ledger support the balance or status      |
| file leave for an employee                | **Leaves** → available apply action                                              | entitlement, shift, dates, supporting records, and workflow     | the application appears in leave history with its status |
| approve or reject a request               | **To Do**                                                                        | employee, date, request, evidence, and earlier approval history | the queue and request status update after confirmation   |

> **access rule:** seeing an employee does not give you every action for that employee. your administrator assigns both the employees in your scope and the functions you can use. an action that is missing may be outside your assigned access even when the employee is visible.

## how the Manager portal modules work together

* **Workforce** identifies the employee, reporting line, employment information, and employee-specific time-entry setup.
* **Shifts** provides the schedule that gives meaning to the employee's timesheet punches, leave dates, attendance results, and overtime.
* **Hours** records and interprets punches against the assigned shift.
* **Leaves** records approved or pending absences and the employee's leave balance.
* **To Do** holds requests waiting for your decision when you are an assigned approver.

a correction in one module does not automatically correct a different record. reassign the shift when the schedule is wrong. correct the timesheet when the shift is right but a punch is missing or incorrect. file or correct the leave request when the employee's absence should be covered by leave.
