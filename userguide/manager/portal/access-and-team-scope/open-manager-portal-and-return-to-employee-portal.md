# open Manager portal and return to Employee portal

sign in using your own Ashima account. open the portal selector and choose **Manager portal**. Ashima opens Manager portal only when your account has an active manager assignment for the company.

to return to your own records, open the account menu at the top of the page and choose **Employee Portal**. your employee records and your manager responsibilities remain separate even though you use one account.

if **Manager portal** is unavailable, confirm that you selected the correct company. ask your administrator to review your manager assignment when the option is still missing. a reporting relationship by itself does not guarantee Manager portal actions; the administrator must also enable the applicable manager access.

## keep your employee and manager work separate

use Employee portal for your own shift, timesheet, leave, pay, and profile. use Manager portal for employees within your assigned scope. before changing a record, confirm both the current company and the employee name shown on the page.
