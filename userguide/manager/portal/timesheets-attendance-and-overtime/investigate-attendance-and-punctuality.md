# investigate attendance and punctuality

choose the page that matches the question:

| question                                                     | page to use                           |
| ------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------- |
| who is currently clocked in?                                 | **Hours → Attendance → Clocked In**   |
| who has no expected time record?                             | **Hours → Attendance → No Show**      |
| who recorded less time than the shift expects?               | **Hours → Attendance → Undertime**    |
| who arrived before the relevant schedule?                    | **Hours → Punctuality → Early Birds** |
| who arrived after the relevant schedule?                     | **Hours → Punctuality → Tardy**       |
| who has work recorded on a rest day?                         | **Hours → Rest Day**                  |
| which expected workdays appear absent, and is leave present? | **Hours → Timesheet Absences**        |

these pages are investigation views. a result can be caused by the assigned shift, punches, approved leave, or an unresolved request. open the employee's shift and timesheet before deciding which record needs correction. export only the filtered result you are authorized to handle.

## use an exception as a starting point

an exception page identifies records that deserve review. it does not by itself mean the employee made an error or tell you which source record needs correction.

1. open the employee's assigned shift for the date.
2. compare every captured punch with the shift's required punch sequence.
3. review approved or pending leave, shift changes, timesheet adjustments, overtime, and rest-day or holiday applications.
4. check whether the timesheet was recalculated after the latest accepted change.
5. correct the owning source record and verify the result in **Timesheet Logs**.

for **Overbreak**, compare the tracked break-out and break-in punches with the break duration allowed by the shift. for **No Show** or absence, confirm whether approved leave or a different date classification should cover the period. for **Tardy** and **Undertime**, review the company grace and rounding rules before explaining the result.
