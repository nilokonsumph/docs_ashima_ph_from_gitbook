# when to ask HR or your administrator for help

ask for help promptly when:

* you cannot activate or recover your account;
* you lose both your authenticator and recovery codes;
* the desktop clock is missing or a kiosk cannot find your employee ID;
* your shift is wrong and the change action is unavailable;
* a timesheet, leave, CTO, or overtime action is disabled without a reason you can resolve;
* expected leave or CTO credit is missing;
* required personal or pay information is read-only or incorrect; or
* payroll is already locked or closed for a correction.

include the page name, date and time, employee number, applicable shift or payroll period, exact message, and a screenshot when allowed by company policy. never send your password, authenticator code, or unused recovery code.

## include enough information to investigate

send the company, page name, affected date or pay period, leave or request type, current status, action you expected, exact message, and a screenshot when company policy allows it. for time questions, include the assigned shift and relevant punches. for balance questions, include the transaction row that changed the amount.

never send your password, authenticator code, or unused recovery code.
