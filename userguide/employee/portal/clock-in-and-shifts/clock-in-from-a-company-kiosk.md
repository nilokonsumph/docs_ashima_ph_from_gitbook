# clock in from a company kiosk

use a kiosk only when your company provides and authorizes the device.

## before you clock

use the kiosk assigned to your workplace. depending on the kiosk setup, identify yourself using the employee number, identifier, or QR code requested on screen. confirm that the kiosk shows your identity and the intended action before submitting.

## use the shared kiosk

1. enter or scan the employee identifier requested by the kiosk.
2. review the action Ashima is about to record.
3. allow the kiosk to capture a photo when required.
4. submit the punch once.
5. wait for the success result and confirm the recorded action and time.

the kiosk can reject a punch when it cannot find your employee identifier, your employee status is inactive, your timesheet is marked **Not required**, it cannot find a work schedule, a photo is required but missing, or the minimum interval between punches has not passed. report the exact message to HR or your administrator.

if the kiosk says another punch is still processing, wait before trying again. repeated punches can create confusion even when Ashima protects against simultaneous requests.
