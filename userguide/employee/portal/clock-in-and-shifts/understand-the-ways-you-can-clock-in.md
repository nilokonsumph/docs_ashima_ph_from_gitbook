# understand the ways you can clock in

Ashima has three employee-operated methods for adding punches to your timesheet in real time. your company chooses which methods are available to you in its time-entry settings.

## compare the three clock-in methods

| method            | where you use it                                                      | what your company can require                                                                       | how to confirm the record                                                                                |
| ----------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **desktop clock** | the clock on Employee Home after you sign in from your own computer   | access for your account, an allowed network address, and an optional or required photo              | wait for the success message, check the captured action and time, then review **Hours → Timesheet Logs** |
| **company kiosk** | a shared device provided by your company                              | your employee identifier or a QR code, an assigned work schedule, and an optional or required photo | wait for the kiosk success result, check the action and time, then review **Hours → Timesheet Logs**     |
| **mobile clock**  | the Ashima mobile clock when your company enables it for your account | device location, an allowed work location, an optional or required photo, and possible approval     | check the submission result and any approval status, then review **Hours → Timesheet Logs**              |

these are the three real-time clocking methods available to employees in Ashima. a **timesheet application** is a request to correct or add a record after the shift; it is not another clock-in method. a timesheet entry added or uploaded by an administrator is also not employee clocking.

## follow the punch sequence required by your shift

use the method your company has enabled for your workplace or assignment. the next available action can be **time in**, **lunch out**, **lunch in**, **1st break out**, **1st break in**, **2nd break out**, **2nd break in**, or **time out**, according to the breaks required by your shift and the punches already recorded. Ashima may prevent another punch until the company-set minimum interval has passed.

when photo capture is enabled, allow camera access and make sure the captured image belongs to the employee making the punch. mobile clocking may also require location access and may restrict punches to a configured work location.

> **always verify:** a button click alone is not proof that your punch was saved. wait for the success feedback, then confirm the captured time shown on the screen and later in **Timesheet Logs**.

## correct a punch that is missing or wrong

do not add repeated punches while you are unsure whether the first action was saved. check **Hours → Timesheet Logs** after the shift. if a required punch is missing or incorrect, use the supported timesheet application after the shift ends. if the assigned schedule is wrong, request a shift change instead of changing your punches to fit the wrong shift.

see [correct a missing or incorrect timesheet punch](../timesheets-attendance-and-overtime/correct-a-missing-or-incorrect-timesheet-punch.md) and [request a shift change](request-a-shift-change.md).
