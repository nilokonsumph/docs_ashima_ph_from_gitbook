# clock in from mobile when it is enabled

use the Ashima mobile clock only when your company has enabled mobile clocking for your account.

## before you clock

turn on device location and allow Ashima to use it. if your company uses a work-location boundary, move within the allowed radius before submitting. allow camera access when a photo is required. mobile punches may be recorded immediately or sent for approval, depending on company settings.

## submit through the mobile clock

1. open the mobile clocking function and allow location access.
2. review the action and the location detected by the device.
3. take a clear photo when photo capture is required.
4. submit the punch once.
5. confirm the recorded action and review whether it is awaiting approval.

mobile clocking can be blocked when your account is not enabled, your employee or timesheet status is inactive, the device cannot determine its coordinates, you are outside the allowed location radius, or a required photo is missing. correct the permission, location, or photo issue identified by the message. if the setting itself is unavailable, contact HR or your administrator.

a submitted mobile punch can enter an approval workflow. check your timesheet and the request status rather than assuming submission alone completed the final record.
