# understand your loans

loan deductions depend on the loan assigned to you, its repayment amount, dates, status, and payroll schedule. Employee portal separates **Government Loans** from **Third Party Deductions**. government loans can include supported SSS and Pag-IBIG/HDMF loan types; a third-party loan is a different lender or company-supported obligation.

## where can I review a loan payment?

open **Deductions → Government Loans** or **Third Party Deductions** to review the loan record available to you. then open the [payslip](understand-your-payslip.md) for a pay date to confirm the amount deducted in that payroll. the loan's assigned repayment amount or balance is not itself proof that a payment was deducted or remitted on that date.

## why does the amount or payday change?

your employer sets the repayment schedule to match your payroll calendar. a government loan can have a remittance amount and coverage dates. a third-party loan can have an amortization amount, first remittance date, number of payments, and a status such as active, paused, or completed. a loan can therefore start later, pause, finish, or appear only on selected paydays.

Mia, a fictional employee, has an SSS loan with a ₱800 scheduled repayment and a separate company loan with a ₱350 repayment for a fixed number of paydays. she sees both loan records, but one payslip may contain only the SSS loan deduction. she checks the next pay date and the company loan's schedule before concluding that a payment was missed. these are illustrative loan terms, not standard Ashima amounts.

## a loan payment is missing or duplicated

compare the loan type and number, pay date, amount, and earlier payslips. send HR or payroll those details. they can check the loan status, coverage, repayment schedule, and whether the pay run was recalculated. if a closed payroll needs correction, changing or deleting today's loan record does not erase the historical deduction.
