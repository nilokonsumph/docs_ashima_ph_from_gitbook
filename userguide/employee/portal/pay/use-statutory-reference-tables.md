# use statutory reference tables

The statutory tables in Employee Portal are guides. They can show the enabled SSS, PhilHealth, HDMF, GSIS, and withholding-tax references, but they do not calculate your individual payroll result.

Open the applicable table and check its period or version before comparing a value. Your employer's contribution basis and deduction schedule affect what appears on a particular [regular payroll payslip](understand-your-payslip.md). For example, an SSS deduction may be split across two payrolls rather than taken in full on one payslip.

If a payslip amount differs from what you expected, send HR or payroll the pay period, contribution or tax name, displayed amount, and the table row you used for comparison. See [contributions and withholding tax](understand-your-contributions-and-tax.md) for help comparing amounts across pay dates.
