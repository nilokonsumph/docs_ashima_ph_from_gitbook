# understand your pay

start with the record that matches the payment you want to check. the Employee portal provides separate views for regular payroll, 13th Month, and payroll-related disbursements. a final payslip is prepared through the employer's termination process; ask HR or payroll how it will be provided to you.

| record                       | when to use it                                                                                            | start here                                                                         |
| ---------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| regular payroll payslip      | check pay for an ordinary payroll period, including earnings, deductions, contributions, tax, and net pay | [understand your regular payroll payslip](understand-your-payslip.md)              |
| 13th Month payslip           | check the separate 13th Month payout and the pay entries used to calculate it                             | [understand your 13th Month payslip](understand-your-13th-month.md)                |
| payroll disbursement payslip | check a payroll-related amount prepared for release outside the ordinary salary payslip                   | [understand your payroll disbursement payslip](understand-payroll-disbursement.md) |
| final or termination payslip | read the final-pay output your employer provides when employment ends                                     | [understand a final payslip](understand-your-final-payslip.md)                     |

an assigned item and a completed payslip answer different questions. **Earnings** and **Deductions** show what has been set up for you; a payslip shows what a particular completed pay run included. if an amount differs from what you expected, compare the item and its dates with the matching payslip.
