# manage businesses, account details, and capacity

Use this area to choose or maintain a business, update account and billing details, and understand company or subscription capacity.

Business creation and administrator assignment come before company payroll configuration. Capacity information explains whether another company or employee population can be added; it does not replace the business and Workforce records themselves.
