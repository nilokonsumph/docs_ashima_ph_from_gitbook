# review plan capacity and company counts

open **Add Ins** to review plan information, administrator and employee counts, and available slots.

**navigation:** **Control Center → Add Ins → Plan Information**

## read the account and company figures

check the displayed owner name/email and plan, then review active and inactive employee totals, administrator count, available slots, and the company breakdown.

available slots are derived from plan capacity minus employee and administrator counts. account totals may cover more companies than an ordinary administrator can see in the company breakdown; do not assume the two views must have the same scope.

## use the screen as information

check that you are reading the intended account before using the displayed capacity figures. **Add Ins** here is Plan Information, not an installation or purchase workflow. counts and slots do not by themselves establish a feature entitlement.
