# update account and billing profile information

use **Account Profile** to update personal/contact information or the company/billing-information section available to you.

**navigation:** **Control Center → Account Profile**

## update the intended section

keep personal details separate from company and billing information.

| section                     | information you can update                                              | check before saving                                             |
| --------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| personal details            | name and mobile fields.                                                 | satisfy the displayed validation.                               |
| company/billing information | company name, address, city, ZIP, website, contact, telephone, and fax. | the section is conditional on owner/designated/support context. |

use **Update** for the intended information and review the saved result or validation feedback. a visible personal section does not guarantee visibility of company/billing fields.

## change your password

use the current-password, new-password, and confirmation fields. the current password is checked; review the result rather than assuming a rejected change was saved.

## keep billing information in scope

this page maintains profile and billing-information fields. it does not provide an invoice, payment, card-management, or plan-purchase procedure, and does not imply that updating profile fields changes all company records.
