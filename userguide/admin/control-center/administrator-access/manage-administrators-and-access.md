# manage administrators and access

## manage administrators and access

use **Teams** to add administrators, enable or disable their accounts, assign companies, and review their permissions.

**navigation:** **Control Center → Teams** for an owner, or **Control Center → Privilege** for a designated administrator

### understand the administrator list

use this table to understand the controls shown for each administrator.

| control                         | what it shows or does                                                         |
| ------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Enable**                      | turns the administrator account on or off.                                    |
| **Roles**                       | shows a short label that helps identify the administrator's responsibilities. |
| **Admin Details** and **Email** | identify the administrator and show invitation information.                   |
| **Assign Companies**            | shows the companies included in the administrator's access.                   |
| **Action**                      | opens the controls for editing or deleting the administrator.                 |

the **Roles** label is only a quick identifier. open the administrator to review the assigned permissions under **Available Roles** and **Selected Roles**.

### add an administrator

1. choose **add new admin**. Ashima opens the **Add New Role** form for the new administrator.
2. enter the administrator's first name, last name, and separate administrator email address.
3. choose or enter the short role label that best describes the administrator's responsibilities.
4. select the companies the administrator needs.
5. choose **Save** and complete any required confirmation.
6. open the new administrator, choose **Edit**, and review the detailed permission labels before relying on the account.

### choose which companies an administrator can access

**List of Companies** controls which businesses under the account the administrator can access. select every company the administrator needs; an administrator can be assigned to more than one company.

company access and permissions are separate:

* **List of Companies** determines which businesses the administrator can open.
* **Available Roles** and **Selected Roles** determine the pages and actions available to that administrator. this can be customized regardless of the label under **Roles**.

review both settings before saving. selecting a company does not grant every page, and changing page access does not add another company.

### review or change administrator access

1. open the intended administrator and choose **Edit**.
2. check the role label, **Selected Roles** permission labels, and **List of Companies** separately.
3. move only the required permission labels between **Available Roles** and **Selected Roles** when the page allows changes.
4. choose **Update**, complete the password confirmation, and review the saved result.

the role label does not grant or explain all access by itself. account state, invitation status, confidentiality rules, Admin My Team Access, Manager Privileges, and Workflow assignments remain separate.

### manage the administrator account

* use **Enable** to activate or deactivate the administrator account.
* use **resend** when an invitation needs to be sent again.
* use **edit admin** to change the administrator's details or access.
* use **delete admin** only after confirming that the account should be removed.

after any change, confirm the administrator's enabled state, invitation state, companies, and required permissions.
