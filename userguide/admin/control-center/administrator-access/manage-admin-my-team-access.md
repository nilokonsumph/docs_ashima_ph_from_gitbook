# manage Admin My Team Access

use **Admin My Team Access** to enable or disable the my team entry path for an eligible administrator. it does not choose the employees the person can see or the actions the person can perform. configure those details through Manager Privileges.

**navigation:** **Control Center → Admin My Team Access**

## understand who appears

the list shows eligible active administrators. designated administrators are not included. if an expected person is missing, check whether the administrator account is active and whether the person is a designated administrator.

## enable or disable access

1. search for the administrator by name.
2. change the **My Team Access** toggle.
3. wait for the saved confirmation.
4. confirm the toggle remains in the intended state.
5. configure or review the person's employee-level Manager Privileges when team population or permitted actions must change.

the toggle saves immediately. if saving fails, the page returns the toggle to its previous state and shows an error.

## review the Audit Trail

use **Filter user...**, the starting date, and the ending date to narrow the **Audit Trail**, then choose **Apply**. the audit entry shows the change date and time, the user who made the change, the target administrator, and the previous and new My Team Access status. choose **Clear** to reset the filters.

## verify or correct the result

confirm both the saved toggle and the new audit entry. if the administrator can open my team but sees the wrong employees or actions, correct Manager Privileges rather than repeatedly toggling Admin My Team Access.
