# review employee disbursement records

use the employee-level **Disbursement** pages to review payroll items, disbursement details, and supported non-payroll items associated with the employee. access remains subject to employee visibility and confidential-record rules.

this Workforce view helps trace the employee record connected to a payment item. creating, importing, submitting, downloading, or correcting the disbursement belongs to the Disbursement module.

verify the employee, item type, amount, status, and current company before using the record as payment evidence. a visible item does not by itself prove that a bank accepted or completed payment.
