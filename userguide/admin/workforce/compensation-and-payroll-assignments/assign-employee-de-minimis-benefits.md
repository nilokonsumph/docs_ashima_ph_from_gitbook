# assign employee De Minimis benefits

use the employee De Minimis pages to assign a company-defined De Minimis item to an employee, review an existing assignment, or confirm the amount that can feed an eligible payroll.

**navigation:** go to **Workforce → De Minimis**

## understand the company item and employee assignment

* the company item in **Settings → Earnings → De Minimis** defines the benefit and its supported tax and payroll treatment.
* the employee assignment connects that item to one employee and records the supported amount, dates, schedule, and status.
* creating or changing a company item does not automatically create or change every employee assignment.

## assign or update the employee benefit

1. open **De Minimis** and find the employee.
2. open the employee detail and review existing assignments before adding another one.
3. choose the intended company item.
4. enter the supported amount, effective dates, schedule, and other required values shown on the form.
5. save the assignment and reopen the employee detail.
6. confirm the item, amount, dates, schedule, and status in a recalculated open payroll before approval.

{% hint style="warning" %}
**warning:** changing an item definition, threshold, or employee amount can change the taxable or non-taxable payroll result. check effective timing and open payroll before saving. a new assignment does not rewrite closed payroll.
{% endhint %}

\## correct an unexpected result

confirm the company item first, then the employee assignment, effective dates, payroll schedule, employee entitlement, and open-payroll calculation. correct the owning record and recalculate the eligible open payroll; do not add an unrelated adjustment merely to conceal an incorrect setup.
