# review salary and compensation history

use compensation history to review effective-dated salary and compensation changes and trace the values used by payroll.

**navigation:** go to **Earnings → Compensation**, open the employee, then review the history section.

## what does compensation history display?

the history records previous and new pay information, the effective date, change description, and available detail action. Salary-increase history supports the current-period transparency and retroactive-pay workflows.

the effective date is important because a change can affect a current payroll, a later payroll, or retroactive-pay treatment. Review the payroll dates and the history entry together.

## how do i review a change?

1. open the employee's compensation details.
2. locate the compensation history.
3. select the relevant change.
4. review the previous pay, new pay, effective date, and recorded context.
5. open the detail when a salary-increase or retroactive breakdown is available.

## what should i compare with payroll?

* the old and new basic-pay values;
* the effective date;
* the employee's payroll group and pay schedule;
* open or closed payrolls covering the effective date;
* any retroactive-pay result; and
* future-pay or special-payroll calculations that use current compensation.

if the payroll still shows the old amount after an eligible change, review recalculation status and the effective date before creating a separate adjustment.

{% hint style="warning" %}
**important:** deleting a compensation-history entry may revert pay to an earlier value and affect payroll. Use the deletion action only after reviewing the affected payroll periods.
{% endhint %}

deletion does not automatically reverse a completed payroll. Use the approved payroll-correction process when a closed result is affected.
