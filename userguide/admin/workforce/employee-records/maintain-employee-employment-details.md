# maintain employee employment details

use employment details to maintain an employee's organization assignment, employment information, government IDs, reporting relationship, and workflow assignments.

{% hint style="warning" %}
**warning:** changing department, position, employment type, reports-to assignment, or workflow group may change access and approval routing. Review the effective details before saving.
{% endhint %}

\*\*navigation:\*\* go to \*\*Workforce → Employment Details\*\*, then open the employee.

## find the employment record to change

the list page provides employee name or ID search, sorting by first name, last name, rank, or employment status, **Excel** and **CSV** downloads, and employee drill-down. It displays job position, rank, cost center, hire and regularization dates, employment type and status, and location. Edit employment values only after opening the employee detail page.

## how do i update employment details?

1. search for the employee.
2. open the employee detail page.
3. select the employment section.
4. click **Edit** when required.
5. update the applicable fields.
6. review any effective date or history entry.
7. click **Save**.

## what can i maintain?

when a relevant application is pending, you cannot move the employee to a workflow group with fewer approval levels. Complete the pending application first, or choose a group with at least the current number of levels.

* company and organization assignment
* department, job position, employment type, and rank where enabled
* date hired and employment dates
* government IDs
* job and employment-type change history
* reports-to relationship
* workflow group and approver assignment

## what happens after saving?

the employee record and related history update. Reporting lines and approval workflows use the new assignment after the applicable change takes effect.
