# maintain employee personal details

use personal details to maintain an employee's profile, notes, emergency contact, dependents, mobile numbers, and account-recovery information.

**navigation:** go to **Workforce → Personal Details**, then open the employee.

## find the personal record to change

the list page provides employee name or ID search, sorting by employee or workforce attributes, **Excel** and **CSV** downloads, and employee drill-down. Changes are made inside the selected employee's detail page.

## how do i update personal information?

1. search for the employee.
2. open the employee detail page.
3. open the personal-information section you need.
4. click **Edit** when the section uses edit mode.
5. update the fields.
6. click **Save**.

## what can i maintain?

* personal and contact information
* employee notes
* emergency contact
* dependents
* additional mobile numbers and the primary mobile number
* password or password-recovery action, when allowed

## which details affect other modules?

* the employee name and contact details appear in workforce lists, documents, and supported employee communications;
* the primary mobile number can be used by supported account and time-entry processes;
* dependents and civil information may be used by employee records and statutory outputs where applicable; and
* account-recovery actions affect how the employee regains portal access.

personal details do not replace Employment Details or Compensation Details. Maintain organization, status, payroll, and salary information on their owning pages.

## what happens after saving?

the employee detail refreshes with the updated values. Changes remain subject to your workforce privileges and visibility scope.

## what should i check when i cannot edit a field?

confirm that you have the required Workforce privilege and access to the employee. Confidential employees require additional access. Some identity or account fields may use a dedicated action instead of ordinary edit mode.
