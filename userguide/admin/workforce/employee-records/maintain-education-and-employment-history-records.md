# maintain education and employment-history records

use the supporting employee pages when the employee's education or a prior employment record must be added, corrected, or reviewed. these records add context to the employee profile; they do not change current compensation, status, or payroll by themselves.

## maintain Educational Background

open the employee record and select **Educational Background**. review the saved education rows, then use the available add or edit action. save the institution, qualification, dates, and other supported details, and reopen the row to verify the result.

## review Employment History

open **Employment Details**, select the relevant history entry, and review the employment-history detail. use the current Employment Details workflow for a current job, employment type, department, reporting-manager, or status change. do not edit an old history row as a substitute for an effective current change.

## why can’t i edit or delete a PMS decision in history?

A **PMS decision** row records an outcome from a performance evaluation. It is read-only in employment history: it does not open the manual history editor and cannot be selected for deletion, even when its effective date is in the future. Use the owning performance-evaluation process to investigate that decision.

For a manual job or employment-type change, use its separate action, enter the effective date and required details, save, and check the reloaded history. Only future-effective records that offer deletion can be selected for that action; a future date alone does not make every history row deletable.
