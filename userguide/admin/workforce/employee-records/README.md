# maintain employee records

Use this area to onboard and find employees, maintain personal and employment information, change employment status, and review the records that explain an employee's place in the organization.

Correct information at the employee record before fixing a dependent Shift, Hours, Leave, Paycheck, report, or portal result. After a material change, review the downstream workflow that consumes it.
