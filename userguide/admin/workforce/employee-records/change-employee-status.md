# change employee status

use employee status to record an employment-status change and control whether an employee remains active in workforce and payroll workflows.

{% hint style="warning" %}
**important:** changing employee status affects payroll, leave, access, and reports. Resolve open payroll inclusion and confirm the effective date before proceeding.
{% endhint %}

\*\*navigation:\*\* open the employee record, then select \*\*Employee Status\*\*.

## review the current employment status

the page shows the employee's current status and available status history or action. The page checks relevant payroll conditions before completing some changes.

the current status update accepts the selected employment status and does not expose an effective-date or required-reason input. Changes to **Inactive** or **End of Employment** are blocked while the employee is included in an open pay run. For **End of Employment**, the update clears the employee's email and mobile credentials.

## how do i update employee status?

1. open **Employee Status**.
2. select the new status.
3. confirm the selected status.
4. review the payroll or access warning.
5. continue to the confirmation step.
6. confirm the update.

## what happens after the update?

the employee's workforce status and history update. Payroll, leave, portal access, and report visibility follow the status and the applicable company rules. For **End of Employment**, the profile remains viewable, but employment and personal-detail changes are disabled. If the employee belongs to an open pay run, complete the required exclusion or recalculation step.

## what should i review before making an employee inactive or separated?

* open or unapproved payroll and special-payroll records;
* pending leave, overtime, shift, and timesheet requests;
* final-pay or termination work that still needs to be completed;
* active earnings, deductions, loans, and benefits;
* employee portal or time-entry access; and
* reports that use an as-of date or employment status.

changing status does not automatically correct completed payroll or replace the termination/final-pay workflow.

## how do i correct the wrong status or date?

review the status history and use the available correction flow. If payroll or reports were already generated from the incorrect status, correct the employee record first, then recalculate or regenerate the affected open outputs.
