# understand employee visibility and missing records

an employee can be present in Ashima without appearing in every administrator or manager view. the current company, Workforce page access, employee scope, confidential access, manager scope, and applicable data-privacy controls determine which employee records the signed-in user can open.

## check why an employee is missing

1. confirm that you are working in the intended company.
2. confirm that the employee exists and review the employee's current status.
3. confirm that the signed-in administrator can access the required Workforce page.
4. check whether the employee is confidential and whether the administrator has the corresponding access.
5. for manager or **My Team** views, confirm the employee's **Reports To** relationship and the saved manager scope.
6. review the applicable data-privacy setting when the page or field is visible but information is redacted or unavailable.
7. sign out and back in, or reopen the page, after an access change when the current session does not immediately reflect it.

{% hint style="info" %}
**note:** page access, company access, employee visibility, and Workflow approval authority are separate controls. changing one does not automatically grant the others.
{% endhint %}
