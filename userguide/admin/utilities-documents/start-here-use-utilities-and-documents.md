# start here: use Utilities and Documents

use Utilities to access disbursement, employee documents, and support resources. Support Access settings are separate from Utilities. The tax-limit page is under **Reports → Tax**; use the [Projection Tool guide](../paycheck/use-projection-tool-v2.md) for withholding estimates and optional payroll adjustments.

**navigation:** go to **Utilities**.

## what tools are available?

* **Disbursement** supports employee payment batches.
* **Documents** assigns and prints supported employee documents.
* **Support** provides support resources; manage Ashima Support Access in **Control Center → Support**.
* **Reports → Tax → 90k** tracks the annual non-taxable 90,000 limit.

the tools shown depend on your access and the company's enabled modules.

## where should i start?

| need                                            | open                                                     |
| ----------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| prepare a separate employee payment             | **Utilities → Disbursement**                             |
| assign or print an employee document            | **Utilities → Documents**                                |
| review the annual non-taxable 90,000 limit      | **Reports → Tax → 90k**                                  |
| review projected withholding tax or adjustments | [Projection Tool](../paycheck/use-projection-tool-v2.md) |
| manage temporary Ashima support access          | **Control Center → Support Access**                      |

## how do i contact support?

open **Utilities → Support** and use the displayed email link to open a message in your mail application. review the available support resources and release-note information on that page.

the page also displays the current Ashima Support Access status and its note. to change support access, use [Control Center](../control-center/).

## what should i check before changing a utility record?

utilities can affect payroll, tax, employee documents, payment files, or support access. Confirm the employee or company scope, reporting year or payment date, related open payroll, and your authority before saving, recalculating, assigning, or downloading.

when a utility exposes information from another module, correct the owning source record first. Regenerate or recalculate the utility output afterward.
