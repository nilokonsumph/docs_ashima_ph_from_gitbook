# definition of terms

## definition of terms

**payroll group** — a payroll-processing definition that groups employees under one pay schedule, calculation type, default assigned work schedule, and confidentiality classification.

**pay schedule** — how often payroll is processed, such as Semi Monthly or Monthly.

**assigned work schedule** — the work schedule stored on a payroll group and used as the default schedule context for employees assigned to that group unless an applicable employee-level schedule is assigned later.

**confidential payroll group** — a payroll group restricted to the matching confidential employee population and administrator access scope.

**pay date** — the scheduled date associated with payment for the payroll result.

**pay period** — the covered date range whose eligible payroll inputs are considered.

**pay run** — a payroll calculation for one payroll group and pay date, covering a defined pay period.

**Pay Run Details** — the employee-level payroll review for one selected pay run.

**Payroll Masterlist** — the consolidated review of included employees, totals, readiness, adjustments, exclusions, and available payroll actions.

**recalculation** — rebuilding payroll results from eligible current inputs after relevant data changes.

**open shift** — work without a fixed scheduled shift whose eligible hours may need to be supplied or confirmed for payroll.

**payroll exclusion** — a date-scoped choice that prevents an employee's payroll from being processed in the matching payroll context without terminating the employee or removing the payroll-group assignment.

**adjustment** — a supported direct payroll correction applied to an open payroll context.

**ingestion** — a direct payroll entry recorded through the Paycheck entry workflow; its fields, sign, and output category depend on the selected item.

**Pay Draft** — an employee-facing preview of open payroll that can still change before finalization.

**P indicator** — the payroll-update readiness check on the Payroll Masterlist.

**T indicator** — the timesheet-readiness check on the Payroll Masterlist.

**Payroll Lock** — a company-level control that pauses covered payroll-sensitive activity, with a separate choice for Admin To-Do decisions.

**PPA** — a post-payroll adjustment that carries an eligible correction from a previously closed payroll into a later open payroll.

**closed payroll** — a payroll whose required final approval has completed and whose normal open-run editing path has ended.

**payslip generation** — the separate action that creates eligible employee payslip records after payroll closes.

**pay-rate type** — whether the employee's salary or rate is recorded by month, day, or hour.

**taxable pay** — earnings included in the applicable withholding-tax calculation.

**non-taxable pay** — earnings excluded from withholding tax under the applicable rule and limits.

**gross pay** — taxable pay plus non-taxable pay before total deductions.

**net pay** — gross pay less total deductions.

**quarter of contingency** — the calendar quarter containing the expected date of delivery used by the maternity calculation.

**semester of contingency** — the six-month period associated with the contingency quarter; the maternity calculation looks to the 12 months immediately before this semester for contribution history.

**Monthly Salary Credit (MSC)** — the salary-credit value derived from an employee's supported SSS contribution record and used in the maternity-benefit calculation.

**daily maternity allowance** — the sum of the selected monthly salary credits divided by 180 days.

**salary differential** — the maternity full-pay amount remaining after the calculated SSS maternity benefit and supported employee premium contributions, with a minimum of zero in Ashima.
