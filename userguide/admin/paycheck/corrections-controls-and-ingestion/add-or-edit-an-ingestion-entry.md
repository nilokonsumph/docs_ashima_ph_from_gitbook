# add or edit an ingestion entry

1. open the adjustment or ingestion action from the owning source record.
2. confirm the source type and record shown by the page.
3. select an eligible employee. termination requires the employee supplied by the termination source.
4. select the available pay category and an item valid for that category.
5. enter the supported date, tax status, amount, hours, rate, or percentage fields shown for the selected item.
6. follow the save process for the owning record and confirm the entry in its saved-ingestion list. Regular Paycheck uses **Review and save → Review entries → Save entries**; see [add adjustments or ingestion](add-adjustments-or-ingestion.md) for the calculator, matching-entry and tax rules. Do not assume that 13th Month, Year End and final pay use that same form.
7. recalculate the owning open record and review the employee result.

the available choices can include configured De Minimis items, allowances, other earnings, service charge, hazard pay, fringe benefits, salary differential, retirement, severance, other deductions, insurance, government or third-party loans, contributions, withholding tax, and 13th Month items. choices vary by source type and company setup. For regular Paycheck, Allowance, Other Earnings and De Minimis tax treatment follows the active item settings and is shown as **(t)/(nt)**.

if the wrong value was saved, edit or delete the ingestion entry while the owning source permits it, then recalculate and verify the source result. do not add a second opposite entry unless that is the approved business correction.
