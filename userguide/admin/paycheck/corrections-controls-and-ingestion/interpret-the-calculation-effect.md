# interpret the calculation effect

ingestion stores a source-linked payroll input. the selected category and item determine whether the amount is treated as an earning, deduction, contribution, withholding-tax value, 13th Month item, or other supported input. tax status and source type further limit the allowed behavior.

verify the effect in the employee detail of the owning open record. if the amount appears under the wrong section or produces an unexpected result, check the selected category, configured item, tax status, date, employee entitlement, and source type; correct the ingestion entry or owning configuration, then recalculate.
