# pay items, contributions, and tax

Use this area to understand the earnings, deductions, statutory contributions, and withholding tax that feed payroll calculations and appear in employee pay details.

Configuration and payroll review have different owners:

* **Settings** owns company item definitions, calculation rules, schedules, and contribution timing.
* **Workforce** owns employee-specific assignments when the item is assigned through the employee record.
* **Paycheck** shows how the configured and assigned items affect the current payroll calculation.
* **Reports** owns statutory and payroll outputs produced from completed records.

When investigating an amount, begin with employee pay details, identify the item and source, then follow the link to its owning configuration or employee assignment. Correct the source and recalculate the open pay run when required.
