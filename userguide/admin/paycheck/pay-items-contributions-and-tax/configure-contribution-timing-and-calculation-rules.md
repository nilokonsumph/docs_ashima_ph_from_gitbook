# configure contribution timing and calculation rules

**navigation:** go to **Settings → Paycheck**, then open **Contribution Schedule**, **Contribution Rules**, or the applicable withholding-tax rule.

**Contribution basis bundles are being prepared for release.** A bundle chooses which payroll-recognized pay amounts form the basis for a contribution; the contribution method and table calculate the amount afterward. The new setup also allows a company default and a different bundle for selected employees, including employees selected together by payroll group. For existing companies, the first step will be to [review how current SSS, PhilHealth, and Pag-IBIG rules migrate](../contribution-basis-bundles/migrate-existing-contribution-rules.md). The [contribution basis bundle guide](../contribution-basis-bundles/) explains how to create and assign bundles. Continue using your current **Contribution Rules** until your company is enabled.

1. open **Contribution Schedule** and select the eligible pay-run timing for each supported contribution.
2. open **Contribution Rules** and review the earnings or adjustments included in each supported calculation base.
3. review the employee's contribution participation choices in Workforce.
4. save the intended company and employee records.
5. recalculate a representative open pay run and confirm both employee and employer portions.

if the result is wrong, restore the intended setting or employee choice and recalculate before approval.
