# understand contributions and compliance

use the Paycheck ecosystem to configure contribution timing and calculation rules, maintain each employee's contribution choices, review deductions in payroll, and generate statutory outputs.

## follow the contribution flow

1. configure the company schedule and calculation rules in Settings.
2. review each employee's SSS, PhilHealth, HDMF, HDMF I, HDMF II, or other supported contribution choices in Workforce.
3. calculate or recalculate the open pay run.
4. review employee and employer contribution amounts before approval.
5. close payroll only after resolving unexpected contribution results.
6. generate and review the applicable statutory report or remittance output.

{% hint style="warning" %}
**warning:** a contribution setting or employee choice can change a future or recalculated open payroll. it does not automatically rewrite approved, closed, filed, or remitted periods.
{% endhint %}
