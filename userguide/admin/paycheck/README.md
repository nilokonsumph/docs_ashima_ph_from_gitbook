# paycheck

Use Paycheck to prepare regular and special payroll, review calculated results, correct supported payroll inputs, complete approval, release payroll outputs, and continue to payment-related work.

Start with [understand Paycheck](start-here-understand-paycheck.md) when you need the payroll lifecycle, prerequisites, and module map.

## regular payroll

* [run payroll](run-payroll/) — move an open pay run through calculation, review, readiness checks, approval, closing, and payslip release.
* [corrections, controls, and ingestion](corrections-controls-and-ingestion/) — correct or import payroll inputs, control payroll-affecting changes, and handle supported late corrections.

## payroll inputs and calculations

* [pay items, contributions, and tax](pay-items-contributions-and-tax/) — trace earnings, deductions, contributions, and withholding tax from their source to the payroll result.
* [contribution basis bundles](contribution-basis-bundles/) — upcoming setup for choosing the pay components used as the basis for SSS, PhilHealth, and Pag-IBIG. The guide includes a migration path for existing contribution rules.
* [salary increases and retroactive pay](salary-increases-and-retroactive-pay/) — process salary changes and supported prior-period differences.

## special payroll processes

* [run and review 13th Month payroll](run-and-review-13th-month-payroll.md)
* [review termination payroll](review-termination-payroll.md)
* [manage maternity benefits](manage-maternity-benefits.md)
* [run and review Year End](run-and-review-year-end.md)

These functions have their own calculation, review, and approval rules. Do not assume the regular-payroll sequence or formula applies without checking the applicable task article.

## planning and payment

* [use Projection Tool](use-projection-tool-v2.md) — create named projections, choose future pay assumptions, review the tax gap, and prepare optional adjustments for an editable pay run. A projection is an estimate, not the final payroll result.
* [disbursement](disbursement/) — prepare, review, approve, and download supported employee payment outputs.
