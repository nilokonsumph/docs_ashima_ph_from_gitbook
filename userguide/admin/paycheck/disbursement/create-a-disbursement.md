# create a disbursement

Create a disbursement when you need to prepare a separate payment for one employee or a selected group of employees.

**navigation:** go to **Utilities → Disbursement**, then select **Disburse**.

## before you begin

confirm the payment date, affected employees, payment classification, amount, tax treatment, and approval requirements.

## fields on the page

* **Payroll group:** narrows the employee list to a payroll group.
* **Department:** narrows the employee list to a department.
* **Cost center:** records the applicable cost center when used by the company.
* **Create Custom List:** lets you select individual employees when the payment is not for a full payroll group or department.
* **Date:** displays the disbursement date for the new run.
* **Voucher number:** records the company's reference number.
* **Confidentiality:** controls the run's confidential classification where available.
* **Payroll Inclusion:** controls whether the payment is included in payroll.
* **Taxable:** controls whether the payment is taxable.
* **Tax Calculation Method:** selects the available tax method when the payment is taxable.
* **Amount:** records the payment amount for the employee.

## how do you create the disbursement?

1. open the add page.
2. select the payroll group, department, or custom employee list.
3. review the date and enter the voucher number.
4. select the payroll-inclusion and tax settings.
5. enter the amount for each employee.
6. review the employee list and classifications.
7. save the disbursement.

## what happens after you save?

Ashima creates a draft and opens it for review. The draft must be recalculated before it is submitted for approval.

## correct a draft

reopen the draft before submission and correct the employee, amount, classification, date, voucher, confidentiality, or tax choice. recalculate and verify the totals. if the draft should not continue, remove the unfinished draft from the list; removing a draft does not cancel an approved or externally paid item.
