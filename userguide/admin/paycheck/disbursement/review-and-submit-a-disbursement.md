# review and submit a disbursement

use the disbursement preview to confirm the employees, amounts, tax classification, and totals before approval.

**navigation:** go to **Disbursement**, then open the applicable draft or record.

## what does the preview show?

the header identifies the disbursement date, total amount, status, voucher number, confidentiality, and payslip-generation status when available. The employee table shows the amount and taxable or non-taxable breakdown per employee.

## what do the statuses mean?

| status                   | meaning and next action                                                                  |
| ------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Open**                 | the disbursement can be reviewed and submitted for approval                              |
| **Waiting for approval** | the disbursement is in its configured approval workflow                                  |
| **Rejected**             | review the result shown on the page; available next actions depend on the current status |
| **Closed**               | the Ashima disbursement workflow is complete and final output actions become available   |

## how do you review and submit it?

1. confirm the disbursement date and voucher number.
2. confirm the confidentiality setting.
3. Search for an employee if you need to check a specific row.
4. review every employee amount and classification.
5. Compare the employee total with the header total.
6. select **Submit for approval**.
7. confirm the submission when prompted.

## can you still edit after submission?

Submission moves the record into the approval workflow. The actions available after submission depend on the current approval status.

## what happens after approval or rejection?

Approval completes the review-stage workflow. **Closed** is the completed disbursement status that enables status-dependent output actions. Rejection shows the workflow result; any next action depends on the current status shown on the page.

## when are payment files and payslips available?

bank-file and cash-file actions are available for a **Closed** disbursement. Payslip generation is also presented after closing when payslips have not yet been generated. Review the closed totals and employee payment information before producing or using these outputs.

in Ashima, **Closed** is the end of the disbursement workflow. It does not independently confirm that an external bank accepted the file or that the employee received the funds.
