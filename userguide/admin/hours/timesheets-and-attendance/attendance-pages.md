# review attendance and absences

## review attendance and absences

use the attendance pages to find employees who are currently clocked in or whose records show no show, undertime, or absence.

**Navigation:**\
go to **Hours → Attendance**, then select the attendance view you need.

### what do the attendance pages display?

* **Clocked In** shows employees with an active time entry for the selected date.
* **No Show** shows scheduled employees without the expected attendance record.
* **Undertime** shows employees whose recorded work ended before the required shift time.
* **Absent** shows scheduled employees without payable work for the reviewed period, subject to leave and attendance rules.

the result depends on the employee's assigned shift, captured time entries, and approved leave. review those sources before treating a row as final.

### how do i review attendance?

1. open the attendance view you need.
2. select the date using the page's calendar control.
3. search for an employee and use the available work schedule, department, or position filters to narrow the list. available filters vary by attendance view.
4. compare the displayed schedule and attendance values with the employee's timesheet.
5. correct the timesheet or review the related leave record when the displayed result is incomplete. on the **Absent** view, use **Show Leave** when you need to include leave context.

For example, if an employee appears in **Absent** but says they were on leave, turn on **Show Leave** and inspect the leave status for that date. Check the assigned shift as well: an unexpected workday can make the date look like a missing attendance record. Confirm the source record and approval before adding a timesheet or changing the shift. Then review the attendance result again.

### how do i download the list?

1. apply the filters you want included in the file.
2. open the download control.
3. select **Excel** or **CSV**.

the download follows the active attendance view and filters.

### what should i check before payroll?

review no-show, undertime, and absent rows before payroll calculation. a missing punch, incorrect shift, pending leave, or unapproved correction can change the employee's payable hours or deductions.

### check leave context on the Absent view

open **Hours → Attendance → Absent** when an employee has no valid attendance for a scheduled date. filter by date, employee, department, and work schedule. Select **Show Leave** to add **Has Leave** and **Filed Minutes** to the results. The lookup helps distinguish a missing timesheet from a date with a filed leave record; it does not change the stored timesheet or leave application. Review the leave's approval and coverage before deciding that the absence is resolved.

an absence listing is a review signal. before adding a time log, confirm the employee's schedule, leave status, employment status, and source system. adding a timesheet for an inactive or **End of Employment** employee is blocked.
