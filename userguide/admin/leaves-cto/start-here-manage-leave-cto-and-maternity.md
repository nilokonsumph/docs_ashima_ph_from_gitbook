### find setup, uploads, approvals, and reports

use the task table above for daily leave and CTO work. use these supporting pages when you need to change the rules, process several records, complete an approval, or retain a report.

| when you need to                                      | go to                                              | why it matters                                                                                              |
| ----------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| create or change an ordinary leave policy             | **Settings → Leaves → Leave Settings**             | defines eligibility, units, accrual, paid treatment, negative balance, lead time, and unused-credit rules   |
| select leave types that employees may convert to cash | **Settings → Leaves → Leave Monetized**            | identifies the eligible leave types and company conversion limit used by the supported monetization process |
| configure Alternative Caregiver Leave                 | **Settings → Leaves → ACL Settings**               | defines the Alternative Caregiver Leave policy and eligible employees                                       |
| configure maternity behavior                          | **Settings → Leaves → Maternity Benefit**          | controls the company maternity options used with employee applications and benefit records                  |
| configure CTO                                         | **Settings → Leaves → CTO**                        | selects eligible approved-work sources, conversion rates, expiry, and who can convert work to CTO           |
| upload assigned or unassigned leave balances          | **Upload Manager → Leave** or **Unassigned Leave** | validates bulk records and directs accepted balances to the appropriate employee leave records              |
| approve a pending leave, CTO, or maternity request    | **To Do's**                                        | opens the request and approver history for the action assigned to you                                       |
| retain a conversion or forfeiture result              | **Reports → Leaves**                               | creates saved reports for the selected leave type, employees, and year or date                              |

the actions shown can vary with company setup, employee eligibility, record status, and administrator access. when an expected action is unavailable, check those conditions in the relevant task article before changing another record.
