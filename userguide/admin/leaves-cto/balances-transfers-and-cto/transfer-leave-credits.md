# transfer leave credits

## transfer leave credits

use leave transfer to move eligible unused credits from one ordinary leave type to another for selected employees.

{% hint style="warning" %}
**warning:** a submitted transfer cannot be undone. pending leave for the affected source may be rejected as part of the transfer, so review the preview before continuing.
{% endhint %}

\*\*Navigation:\*\*\ go to \*\*Leaves → Leave Transfer\*\*.

### how do i prepare a transfer?

1. select the payroll group and employee scope.
2. select the source leave type.
3. select the destination leave type.
4. choose the balance and transfer rule.
5. enter the maximum or custom date when the selected rule requires it.
6. click **Submit** to generate the preview.

### how do i verify the transfer preview?

the preview shows each employee's current source and destination balances, the transfer amount, and the expected new balances. rows that do not meet the transfer rule appear as not transferable.

### how do i complete the transfer?

1. review the preview.
2. select the employees to include.
3. download the preview if you need an offline review.
4. click **Proceed to Transfer**.
5. review the warning and enter the required confirmation.
6. continue with the transfer.

### what happens after transfer?

the selected source balances decrease and the destination balances increase. the ledger records the movement for each employee. generate a new preview whenever you change the filters or transfer rule.

if a row is not transferable, return to the preview and review the source type, destination type, employee eligibility, available balance, pending applications, and selected transfer rule. do not repeat a completed transfer to correct an unexpected result; reconcile the transaction ledger and escalate the record for correction.
