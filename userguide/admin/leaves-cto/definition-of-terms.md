# definition of terms

| term                        | meaning                                                                                                           |
| --------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **leave type**              | reusable company policy for a category such as vacation or sick leave                                             |
| **leave assignment**        | employee-specific connection to a configured leave type, including its effective date and opening position        |
| **earned leave**            | credits earned or added to the employee's balance                                                                 |
| **adjustment leave**        | manual increase or decrease recorded with a reason                                                                |
| **used leave**              | credits consumed by leave or entered as prior use                                                                 |
| **available balance**       | credit currently usable after earned, adjusted, reserved, approved, used, and expired amounts are applied         |
| **CTO**                     | compensatory time off credited from eligible approved work instead of paying the same source work through payroll |
| **CTO pool**                | credit bucket with its source, minutes, allocations, expiry, and status                                           |
| **created CTO credit**      | CTO minutes successfully added from one eligible approved work record                                             |
| **conversion rate**         | multiplier that changes eligible source-work minutes into CTO minutes for a specific work context                 |
| **expiry date**             | boundary after which a credit pool is no longer eligible under the configured rule                                |
| **transaction ledger**      | chronological record used to reconcile leave balance movements                                                    |
| **semester of contingency** | two consecutive quarters ending in the quarter of childbirth, miscarriage, or emergency termination of pregnancy  |
| **MSC**                     | monthly salary credit used by the SSS benefit computation                                                         |
| **ADSC**                    | average daily salary credit calculated from the six highest applicable MSC values divided by 180                  |
