# accept an invitation and activate your account

use the invitation link sent by Ashima or your administrator to set the password for a new account. the link identifies the invited email address and must still be valid when opened.

1. open the invitation link from the intended recipient's email.
2. confirm the name and email shown on the page.
3. enter and confirm a password that passes the account's current password policy.
4. submit the form.
5. sign in with the invited email and the new password.

if the page shows **invalid token**, the invitation cannot be completed from that link. ask an authorized administrator to resend the invitation and use the new link.

{% hint style="warning" %}
**important:** creating or assigning an administrator and delivering an invitation are separate results. a saved administrator record does not by itself prove that the email was delivered or that the recipient activated the account.
{% endhint %}
