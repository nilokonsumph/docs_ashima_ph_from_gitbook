# getting started

Use this section when you are accessing the Ashima Admin portal for the first time, activating an invitation, or recovering access. Continue to **Control Center** to create or select the business and establish administrator access before configuring payroll.

**Navigation:** Sign in to the Ashima Admin portal, then open the guide section that matches the Ashima module you are using.

## how do the modules work together?

Ashima uses connected records. Employee and company settings influence shifts, time, leave, payroll, payment, and reporting.

1. create or choose the business and establish administrator access in **Control Center**.
2. configure the company rules and workflows in **Settings**.
3. maintain the employee's Workforce, compensation, assignment, and access records.
4. assign shifts and review Hours and Leave information.
5. calculate, review, and approve regular or special payroll in **Paycheck**.
6. continue to **Paycheck → Disbursement** or another supported payment output when applicable.
7. generate reports from the completed source records.

correct information at its owning source page. Recalculate payroll or projections and regenerate reports after a source correction.

## how should i use warnings and status information?

read the prerequisites and warning notes before saving, deleting, recalculating, submitting, approving, closing, importing, or downloading. Draft, waiting-for-approval, rejected, approved, and closed records can allow different actions.

high-impact actions such as payroll closing, tax adjustments, employee separation, bulk imports, and payment-file generation should be reviewed by an authorized administrator before completion.

## why might my page look different?

available pages, fields, and actions depend on company setup, enabled modules, employee scope, confidential access, page privileges, record status, and Workflow assignment.
