# sign in and recover access

sign in with the email address registered for the account. available companies, portals, pages, and actions depend on the account, company assignment, access settings, confidentiality, employee or manager scope, workflow assignment, and authentication requirements.

## request a password reset

1. select **forgot password?** from the sign-in or reset page.
2. enter the registered email address.
3. submit the request.
4. open the password-reset link received by email.
5. enter and confirm the new password, then submit.
6. return to sign-in and use the new password.

if the page shows **reset link expired**, request a new link. use the most recent reset email and complete it before the new link expires.

the new password must satisfy the current account password policy. depending on the company and account configuration, sign-in can also require email verification or two-factor authentication.

## check the correct context after sign-in

1. confirm the business or company shown in the header.
2. switch to the intended company when the account has access to more than one.
3. confirm whether the current session opened the Admin, Employee, or Manager portal.
4. if a page or action is missing, review saved access and scope instead of assuming the record was deleted.
