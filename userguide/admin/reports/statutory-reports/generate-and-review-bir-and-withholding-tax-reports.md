# generate and review BIR and withholding-tax reports

use BIR and Withholding Tax reports to review tax information produced from employee and payroll records.

> confirm employee tax details, taxable pay, withholding tax, prior-employer information, and year-end results before using a tax report or certificate.

**navigation:** go to **Reports → BIR** for **1601C** or **2316**. Use **Contributions → Withholding Tax** for the current withholding-tax listing.

## what is the withholding tax report for?

the Withholding Tax listing supports employee search and sorting by first name, last name, or pay date. It displays tax method, tax schedule, and tax withheld when matching data is available.

## what is BIR 1601C for?

BIR 1601C uses saved report information and provides detail, preview, and Excel download actions. Review the covered period and totals before using the output.

## what is BIR 2316 for?

BIR 2316 prepares employee tax-certificate information. The released flow supports generating report rows, opening employee details, updating supported information, downloading the report, and producing employee PDF outputs.

## how do you review a tax report?

1. select the required report and period.
2. Generate or save the report.
3. review the company and employee information.
4. Compare taxable pay and withholding tax with payroll and year-end results.
5. Check prior-employer information where applicable.
6. open individual employee details when available.
7. Download or print the final output only after review.

## correct an unexpected tax report

check the employee's tax status, government identifiers, taxable earnings, prior-employer values, tax withheld, reporting period, and source payroll. correct the owning record, recalculate eligible open payroll when required, then regenerate and review the report before filing or distribution.
