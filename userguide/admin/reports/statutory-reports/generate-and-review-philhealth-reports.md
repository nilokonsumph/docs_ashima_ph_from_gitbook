# generate and review PhilHealth reports

use PhilHealth reports to review contribution and employee information prepared from Ashima records.

{% hint style="warning" %}
**important:** confirm PhilHealth numbers, contribution settings, employment status, and finalized payroll results before using an output for filing.
{% endhint %}

\*\*navigation:\*\* go to \*\*Reports\*\*, then open the required \*\*PhilHealth\*\* report.

## which PhilHealth reports are available?

* **RF1:** employee contribution information for the selected reporting period.
* **ER2:** employee registration or employment information supported by the report.

## how do you prepare a report?

1. select RF1 or ER2.
2. enter the required period and report information.
3. save the report.
4. open its detail or preview.
5. review employee PhilHealth numbers and amounts.
6. Compare contribution totals with payroll.
7. Download the available output.

## what should you do when details are wrong?

correct the employee record or payroll source first. Generate a new or updated report after the correction; do not rely on manual changes to a downloaded file as the only correction.

## why might an employee be missing?

check the employee's PhilHealth number, entitlement, employment status, payroll inclusion, contribution result, and the report period. Also confirm that your access includes the employee and that the payroll supplying the contribution has reached the status required by the report.

## does downloading complete filing?

no. Ashima prepares the available report from its records. The administrator remains responsible for reviewing the output and completing the required submission or payment through the applicable external process.
