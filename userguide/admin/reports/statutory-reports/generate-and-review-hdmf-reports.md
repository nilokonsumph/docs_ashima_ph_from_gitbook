# generate and review HDMF reports

use HDMF reports to review employee contribution and loan-remittance information prepared from Ashima records.

{% hint style="warning" %}
**important:** confirm HDMF numbers, contribution and loan settings, and finalized payroll amounts before using a file for payment or submission.
{% endhint %}

\*\*navigation:\*\* go to \*\*Reports\*\*, then open the required \*\*HDMF\*\* report.

## which report types are available?

the HDMF report area provides contribution-remittance and short-term-loan remittance outputs. Use the contribution report for employee and employer contributions and the STL report for supported HDMF loan deductions.

available formats and bank-specific choices depend on the report and company setup. Select the report by its purpose rather than assuming that one output includes both contributions and loans.

## how do you prepare an HDMF report?

1. open the required HDMF report.
2. select the reporting period.
3. Complete the required employer or payment details.
4. save the report.
5. open the detail or preview.
6. review employee HDMF numbers and amounts.
7. Compare totals with payroll deductions and contributions.
8. Download the required Excel or available bank-format output.

## why are several download formats available?

the report may provide different bank formats for remittance. Select only the format required by the company's payment process.

## what should i check when an employee or amount is missing?

* the employee has a valid HDMF number;
* the employee is entitled and assigned to the applicable contribution or loan;
* the selected reporting period includes the payroll deduction;
* the payroll result is calculated and in the required status;
* the employee is included in the selected payroll scope; and
* your access includes the employee.

correct the employee, loan, contribution, or payroll record at its source, then regenerate the report. A downloaded remittance file is an output; it is not proof that the bank or HDMF accepted the payment.
