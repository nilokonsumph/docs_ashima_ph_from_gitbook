# start here: generate and review reports

use Reports to review, save, and download payroll, statutory, workforce, attendance, leave, and audit information.

Reports organize information from records already stored in Ashima. Correct errors at the source, then regenerate the affected report when needed.

**navigation:** go to **Reports**, then select the required report.

## what report groups are available?

* Payroll reports, including Payroll Register, Payroll Variance, Accounting Entries, Cost Structure, and Withholding Tax.
* Government reports for SSS, PhilHealth, HDMF, and BIR.
* Attendance and paid-hours reports.
* Workforce and employee reports.
* Audit and history reports.
* Leave conversion reports.

available reports depend on your privileges and company setup.

## what is the common report workflow?

1. open the required report.
2. select the period, pay run, employee scope, or other available filters.
3. Generate or save the report.
4. open its detail or preview page when available.
5. review employee counts, totals, and period information.
6. Download the required format.

## why can a report change?

A report can change when its source data changes. Common causes are payroll recalculation, corrected time logs, updated employee information, approved adjustments, or a change in payroll status.

## what should you check when a report looks incomplete?

* selected period or pay run
* employee and payroll-group filters
* report status and recalculation state
* employee government IDs or bank details
* your employee visibility and confidential access
* source records changed after the report was generated
