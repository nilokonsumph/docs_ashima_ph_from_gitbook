# generate leave-conversion reports

use Leave Conversion reports to review leave credits prepared for conversion according to the company's leave policy.

> confirm the leave type, employee balance, conversion rule, and covered period before generating or regenerating a conversion report.

**navigation:** go to **Reports**, then select **Leave Conversion**.

## what does the page support?

the live menu exposes **Leave Conversion** and **Forfeiture**. The current pages identify these saved-report flows as beta, support report-name search and **Create New**, and display leave type, employee selection, date/year basis, and selection-dependent delete controls. Detail, regeneration, and download actions remain available according to the saved report and configuration.

Forfeiture report creation requires a report name, leave type, employee scope, and either a year or specific date. A successfully generated report appears in the saved-report list. To remove a disposable or obsolete saved report, select its checkbox, click **Delete**, and confirm **Yes**.

## how do you prepare the report?

1. open Leave Conversion.
2. select the applicable leave type or supported report option.
3. select the employee scope.
4. confirm the covered period and conversion settings.
5. Generate or regenerate the report.
6. open the report detail.
7. Compare the listed credits with employee leave balances.
8. Download the reviewed report.

## what should you do if a balance is wrong?

review the employee's leave balance, transaction ledger, pending applications, expiry, and adjustments. Correct the source before regenerating the conversion report.
