# generate audit, history, and leave reports

Use this area to review change history and produce leave conversion or forfeiture outputs.

Audit records explain who changed a supported record and when. Leave outputs depend on the configured leave rules, employee balances, and selected period. Verify the source and filters before interpreting a missing or unexpected line.
