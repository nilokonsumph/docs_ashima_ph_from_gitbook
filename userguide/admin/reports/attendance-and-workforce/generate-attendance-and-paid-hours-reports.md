# generate attendance and paid-hours reports

use attendance reports to compare timekeeping records, scheduled work, and paid payroll hours.

**navigation:** go to **Reports → Hours**, then select **Attendance** or **Paid Hours**. **Log Summary** is available under **Reports → Others**.

## what is the attendance report for?

the Attendance Report summarizes attendance for a selected employee or organizational scope and date range. use it to review attendance across the period before comparing it with payroll.

1. create a report with its name, employee or organizational scope, and a date range allowed by the form.
2. select **Save**, correct any validation errors, and open the saved report. select **Cancel** to discard an unsaved form.
3. check the saved period, employee scope, and attendance values.
4. choose **XLS** or **CSV**, completing any offered download checks and extra-column selections.
5. check the downloaded file's coverage before relying on it.

for a whole-scope report, a separate breakdown download is available in **XLS** or **CSV**. it is not available for an individual employee report.

## what is log summary for?

Log Summary presents dated administrator activity for selected administrators and modules. it is not an employee punch report. see [how to review administrator activity](../audit-history-and-leave/review-audit-and-history-reports.md#how-do-i-use-log-summary).

for employee clock-in, clock-out, or break records, use **Hours → Timesheets**, the punch-level **Source**, and timesheet history.

## what is paid hours reconciliation for?

Paid Hours Reconciliation helps compare paid payroll hours with timekeeping information. use it when the payroll hours need to be traced to logs, shifts, or approved requests.

1. create a report with its name and employee or organizational scope.
2. choose **Single Pay Date** for one payroll date, or **Pay Date Range** when that option is available for your scope. select the required date or both range endpoints from the available company dates.
3. select **Save**, correct any errors, and open the saved detail. select **Cancel** to discard unsaved creation.
4. review the saved scope and paid-hours totals, then use **Excel** or **CSV**, completing the download check.
5. investigate any differences using the steps below before preparing fresh output.

## how do you investigate a difference?

1. confirm the report period and employee scope.
2. Check the employee's saved time logs.
3. Check the shift assigned for each affected date.
4. review approved time, overtime, rest-day, holiday, and leave requests.
5. Compare the result with the calculated pay run.
6. correct the source record when needed.
7. Recalculate payroll and regenerate the report.

## can i delete a report without deleting attendance?

select the saved report rows you no longer need and confirm **Delete**. this removes those report records, not the employee's source time logs or payroll. keep reports required for your review process.
