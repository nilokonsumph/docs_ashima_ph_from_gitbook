# generate Workforce reports

use Workforce reports to review employee lists and headcount. Compensation History is under Pay Run reports, while Shift History is under Shifts reports.

**navigation:** go to **Reports**, then select the required Workforce report.

## which workforce reports are available?

* Employee List
* Headcount
* Compensation History
* Shift History

**Employee List** and **Headcount** describe the workforce population for the selected scope. **Compensation History** explains effective-dated pay changes. **Shift History** explains schedule changes. Choose the report that owns the question instead of treating all four as interchangeable employee lists.

## how do you use a workforce report?

### prepare an Employee List

1. create the report, enter its name, and choose the **Narrow by** scope.
2. select **Save** and open the saved report. correct any returned errors, or select **Cancel** to discard the unsaved form.
3. confirm the employee, status, date, and access scope. apply or clear search and organizational filters as needed.
4. choose **XLS**, **CSV**, or **Preview** when available to your account, using the intended report filters.

### prepare a Headcount report

1. enter the report name and **From/To** dates, then select **Save**. correct validation errors or select **Cancel**.
2. review the saved detail's department, rank, employment-type, and cost-center summaries and matrices.
3. choose **XLS** or **CSV** for that saved report.

in either saved-report list, select reports you no longer need and confirm **Delete**. deleting a report does not delete employee records.

### review Shift History

open **Reports → Shifts → Shift History**, choose the date range and employee/scope filters, then search. clear the filters when you need a broader review. read the recorded shift changes and choose **XLS** or **CSV** to export the filtered results.

for effective-dated pay changes, follow [Compensation History](../audit-history-and-leave/review-audit-and-history-reports.md#how-do-i-generate-a-compensation-history-report).

## why can employee counts differ between reports?

Reports can use different dates, statuses, or employee scopes. Confidential access and manager visibility can also limit which employees you see.

check whether the report is effective as of one date or covers a range, whether inactive or separated employees are included, and whether organization filters such as payroll group, department, location, or employment status are active.

## where should corrections be made?

correct employee details in Workforce, compensation changes in Compensation Details, and shift records in Shifts. Regenerate the affected report afterward.

do not edit a downloaded file as the only correction. That file will diverge from Ashima and the next generated report will still use the unchanged source record.

## verify the Workforce output

confirm the selected company, date or period, employee status, visibility scope, and expected employee count. the generated result should reflect the saved Workforce records within that scope. correct personal, employment, status, or organizational assignments in Workforce, then regenerate the report.
