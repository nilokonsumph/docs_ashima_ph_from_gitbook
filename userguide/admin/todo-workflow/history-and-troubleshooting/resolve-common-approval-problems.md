# resolve common approval problems

## resolve common approval problems

### why can i see a request but not approve it?

check the approver level and whether it is currently your turn. view-all access can expose a request without granting the current action. also check your privilege, company, confidentiality scope, request status, Payroll Lock, closed-payroll state, and whether another approver already completed the level.

### why is a request missing?

clear search and filters, confirm the company, open the correct category, and check whether the request was submitted. then review the employee's active Workflow assignment, process type, current approver, module availability, confidentiality scope, and source status.

### why can i not reassign an employee?

the employee may have a pending request and the target Workflow may have fewer approval levels. complete the pending request or choose a compatible target with at least the current number of levels. also confirm that the target Workflow belongs to the correct process and scope.

### why can i not delete a Workflow?

the default Workflow is protected, and an active Workflow can have employee assignments or pending work. review the lock indicator, assigned employees, and current requests. move eligible assignments safely before deleting a non-default Workflow.

### why did an approved value not appear in payroll?

confirm final approval, the source-module status, covered payroll dates, applicable settings, and whether the open pay run was recalculated after the approval. approval and recalculation are separate events.

### why did an email link or notification not complete the task?

a notification or approval link is an entry point. verify the saved decision and source status in Ashima. receipt or opening of a message does not prove that an action completed.
