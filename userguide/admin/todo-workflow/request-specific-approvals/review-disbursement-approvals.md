# review disbursement approvals

## review disbursement approvals

use **Todo → Disbursement** to review a submitted employee payment batch before it advances or closes.

review the disbursement date, voucher or reference, employee scope, payroll group or department, confidentiality, amount per employee, total amount, taxable or non-taxable classification, payroll inclusion, tax method, supporting reason, and latest recalculation time.

compare the header total with the employee rows before deciding. after final approval, the owning Disbursement module provides the applicable payment, bank, cash, report, or payslip actions. rejection records a rejected result; correction and resubmission must be completed from the eligible source or draft flow.

{% hint style="warning" %}
**warning:** approval is not confirmation that a bank accepted or delivered a payment. verify generated files and the applicable payment result separately.
{% endhint %}
