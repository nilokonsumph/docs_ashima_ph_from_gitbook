# review Workforce requests

## review workforce requests

use the applicable queues to decide employee time, schedule, leave, and maternity requests before they affect attendance, balances, or payroll.

### review Timesheets, Shifts, and Mobile Clock-in

compare the original and requested time or shift, assigned schedule, reason, captured logs, overlapping leave or premium work, and open-payroll impact. **Timesheets**, **Shifts**, and **Mobile Clock-in** support actionable selections. **Open Shifts** is review-only in the current Todo page.

an approved time or schedule correction changes the accepted source used by attendance and can affect an open payroll. recalculate the affected pay run after the source result changes. when a timesheet belongs to a closed payroll, approval is blocked; use the supported cleanup action only after reviewing the affected records.

### review Leave and Maternity Benefits

for Leave, confirm the employee, Leave type, covered dates, duration, available balance, schedule, overlaps, reason, and attachments. for Maternity Benefits, review the application, contribution basis, covered period, calculation, documents, and reimbursement-related information applicable to the record.

the current Leave and Maternity Benefits confirmation dialogs do not provide a separate note or reason field. approval changes the applicable Leave, attendance, benefit, or payroll state. rejection follows the source-module recovery path; verify the resulting balance or benefit record rather than assuming the queue alone completed every downstream update.
