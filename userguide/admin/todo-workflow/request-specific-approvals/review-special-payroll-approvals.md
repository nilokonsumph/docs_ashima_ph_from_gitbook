# review special-payroll approvals

## review special-payroll approvals

use **Todo → 13th Month**, **Year End**, or **Termination** to review high-impact payroll results before approval.

confirm the year or period, termination date where applicable, employee scope, inclusions and exclusions, recalculation status, employee detail, adjustments, tax or contribution values, ingestion inputs, available reports, and totals. use the owning module's detailed guide to understand each calculation.

13th Month and Termination confirmations do not provide a reason field. Year End rejection supports remarks. do not invent or expect a reason field when the selected confirmation does not show one.

approval advances or completes the configured Workflow. a rejected result must be corrected and recalculated in the owning module before resubmission. the queue does not repair the calculation.
