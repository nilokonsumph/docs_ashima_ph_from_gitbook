# dashboard

use the admin dashboard to see current payroll, attendance, leave, holiday, and workforce reminders in one place.

Dashboard cards are summaries. Open the related page when you need to investigate or correct a record.

**navigation:** go to **Dashboard**.

## what can the dashboard show?

depending on company setup and your access, the dashboard may show:

* Clocked-in employees
* Early birds
* No show
* Tardy employees
* Undertime
* Employees out on leave
* Next payroll
* Payroll breakdown
* Upcoming holiday
* Birthdays and work anniversaries
* Company announcements

## what is Dashboard Wizard?

Dashboard Wizard is a setup and help flow for payroll-calendar and payroll-group setup. It also includes steps for assigning shifts and enabling Hours.

Use it only after the business exists and the administrator has access to that business. See [complete initial payroll setup](complete-initial-payroll-setup.md) for the guided steps and prerequisites.

## how should you use the attendance cards?

1. review cards with unexpected counts.
2. open the related Hours page from the card when available.
3. Check the employee's time logs and assigned shift.
4. confirm whether a pending adjustment or approval explains the result.
5. correct the source record rather than treating the dashboard count as the final record.

## why can two administrators see different results?

Dashboard visibility can depend on role, assigned privileges, manager scope, and confidential-employee access. A hidden card or employee does not always mean the underlying record is missing.

## when do dashboard values change?

Values update when their source records change. Examples include new time logs, approved leave, updated payroll dates, holidays, employee details, and announcements.

## review birthdays and work anniversaries

The **Birthdays** and **Anniversaries** cards help administrators see employees celebrating during the current month. Open the applicable card when you need the detailed list.

The Birthdays page shows the employee, position, and date of birth for employees with birthdays in the current month. The Anniversaries page shows the employee, position, and date hired for employees with work anniversaries in the current month. Employees hired during the current year are excluded from the anniversary list.

### why is an employee missing from the list?

1. Confirm that the employee is active.
2. Check the employee's date of birth or date hired in **Workforce**.
3. Confirm that you have permission to view the employee.
4. Check whether confidential-employee access limits the result.
5. Return to the Dashboard after correcting the source record.

The cards use saved Workforce information. Correct an inaccurate date in the employee record rather than attempting to edit it from the Dashboard list.

## how do i follow up on a Leave Conversion reminder?

when a **Leave Conversion** reminder appears, select **Click Here** to open the relevant **Leave Settings** detail and review that leave type.

{% hint style="info" %}
opening the reminder does not convert leave or change a leave balance. reminder availability depends on company setup.
{% endhint %}
