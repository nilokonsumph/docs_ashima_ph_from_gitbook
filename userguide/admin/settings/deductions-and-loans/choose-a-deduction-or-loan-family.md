# choose a deduction or loan family

use Deductions settings to define reusable deduction and loan types, then assign them to employees through the matching employee page. This overview helps you choose the correct family.

**navigation:** go to **Settings → Paycheck → Deductions**, then select the required family.

## which family should i use?

| family           | use it for                                                                          | employee record normally includes                                                            |
| ---------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| government loan  | an SSS or HDMF loan type supported by the government-loan workflow                  | loan number, loan type, remittance due, payment schedule, and dates                          |
| third-party loan | another approved lender, company loan, or third-party loan type                     | loan number, total payable amount, amortization method, schedule, and dates or payment count |
| insurance        | a recurring or one-time insurance deduction                                         | insurance item, amount, recurrence, schedule, and dates                                      |
| other deduction  | an approved deduction that is not a government loan, third-party loan, or insurance | reason, amount, recurrence, schedule, and dates                                              |

Government loans and third-party loans are the two main loan families. Do not use Other Deductions merely to bypass the balance, amortization, and reporting meaning of a loan.

## common setup-to-payroll flow

1. create the company item under **Settings → Paycheck → Deductions**.
2. Define its applicability and available schedule.
3. save the item.
4. open the matching employee deduction or Workforce loan page.
5. Assign the item to the employee.
6. review amount or balance, schedule, dates, and status.
7. Recalculate any affected open payroll.
8. confirm the deduction on employee pay details, payslip, and applicable reports.

creating a setting does not immediately deduct an amount from every applicable employee.

## before changing an item already in use

* review current employee assignments;
* review remaining loan balances or occurrences;
* identify open payroll using the item;
* preserve completed-payroll history and reporting meaning; and
* create a replacement item when the business meaning changes materially.
