# configure insurance deductions

use Insurance settings to define a reusable insurance deduction, then assign it to employees through the Insurance deduction page.

**navigation:** for setup, go to **Settings → Paycheck → Deductions → Insurance**. for assignment, go to **Deductions → Insurance**, then open the employee.

## before you begin

* confirm the provider or insurance name;
* select the insurance type and record the plan details;
* confirm the employee deduction amount;
* decide whether the deduction is one-time or recurring;
* choose interval or occurrence scheduling when recurring; and
* review whether the provider already exists as an active item.

Active insurance names must be unique.

## how do i create an insurance item?

1. open **Settings → Paycheck → Deductions → Insurance**.
2. select the add action.
3. enter the insurance name or provider.
4. select the insurance type. If **Others** is selected, enter the requested type.
5. Add the plan details.
6. enter the deduction amount.
7. choose whether the deduction is recurring.
8. for recurring insurance, choose:
   * **Interval**, then complete deduction schedule, payroll schedule, start date, and end date; or
   * **Occurrence**, then complete occurrence count, payroll schedule, and start date.
9. for non-recurring insurance, complete the displayed one-time date.
10. review and save.

## how do i assign insurance to an employee?

1. open **Deductions → Insurance**.
2. Search for the employee and open the employee detail.
3. select **Assign Insurance**.
4. select the insurance item.
5. confirm the amount, recurrence type, schedule, and dates.
6. save the assignment.
7. confirm the assignment appears in the employee list.
8. Recalculate and review the affected open payroll.

the available payroll-schedule choices are checked against the employee's payroll calendar. Use the employee's valid schedule rather than forcing an unsupported payroll position.

## how do i manage an existing insurance deduction?

* Edit an active eligible assignment when the provider and plan meaning remain the same.
* use the end date or occurrence count to complete a finite deduction.
* a completed insurance item cannot be edited through the current employee page.
* Delete only an eligible record after confirming that open payroll does not depend on it.
* Removing a current assignment does not rewrite a completed payroll.

## warnings and troubleshooting

* **Insurance Name must be unique:** search active providers before adding another definition.
* **Unsupported payroll schedule:** confirm the employee payroll group and choose an available payroll schedule.
* **Completed record cannot be edited:** create the approved new assignment or correction rather than altering the completed record.
* **Missing from payroll:** check status, schedule, dates, amount, and recalculation.
* **Wrong provider or plan:** do not rename a historically used item to represent a different insurance product.
