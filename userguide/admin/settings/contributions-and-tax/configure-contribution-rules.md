# configure contribution rules

use contribution settings to control how SSS, PhilHealth, and HDMF contributions are calculated, scheduled, and adjusted in payroll.

{% hint style="warning" %}
**warning:** contribution changes affect statutory deductions and reports. Review the effective payroll period and recalculate open payroll before approval.
{% endhint %}

\*\*navigation:\*\* go to \*\*Settings → Paycheck → Contribution\*\*.

## what are the contribution setting areas?

* **Calculation** controls the pay basis and available calculation choices.
* **Schedule** controls which payroll or cutoff deducts the contribution.
* **Rules** controls available adjustments or company-specific contribution behavior.
* contribution tables provide the applicable statutory lookup values used by payroll.

Ashima is preparing [contribution basis bundles](../../paycheck/contribution-basis-bundles/) for SSS, PhilHealth, and Pag-IBIG. If your company already has **Yes** selections in Contribution Rules, the new guide explains how to review them before migration. Continue using your current rules until the new calculation is enabled for your company.

## how do i update a contribution rule?

1. open the applicable contribution area.
2. select SSS, PhilHealth, or HDMF where required.
3. review the current calculation and schedule.
4. update the available option.
5. confirm any employee-level voluntary or modified setting that remains applicable.
6. click **Save**.

## what happens after saving?

future payroll calculation uses the updated rule for eligible employees. Contribution reports use finalized payroll values, so do not treat a settings preview as a remittance report.

## recover from an incorrect contribution result

verify the rule in a controlled payroll calculation for an eligible employee. if the result is wrong, restore the supported rule or correct the employee contribution profile, then recalculate the open payroll. do not use a current settings change as evidence that submitted, approved, closed, or historical payroll was corrected.
