# set up mobile clock-in

To enable mobile clock in go to **Settings > Hours > Time Entry > Mobile** in the admin panel.

* Turn **ON Mobile Clock-In** to allow employees to use the ashima timekeeping app on iPhone or Android devices to clock IN and Out.

## can i require approval for mobile time logs?

**Yes.**

* Toggle **Require Approval of Time Logs** to ON.
* when enabled, every mobile punch must be reviewed and approved before it becomes a valid record.
* if disabled, logs are automatically accepted once submitted.

## how do i enable photo capture for mobile clock-in?

1. in the mobile clock-in settings, turn ON **Clock-In Photo Capture.**
2. once enabled, a photo will be required each time an employee clocks in or out.
3. photos are stored for up to 3 months with the timesheet records for reference.

## how do i set up geolocation fencing?

1. in the mobile clock-In settings, enable **Geolocation Fencing.**
2. enter the required details:
   * **latitude** of the job site.
   * **longitude** of the job site.
   * **radius (in meters)** – the maximum distance employees must be within to clock in/out.
3. save your settings.

employees outside the defined radius will not be able to clock in or out.

## verify mobile capture

use a permitted test employee to record a mobile clock action with the configured photo, geolocation, and approval rules. verify the request or time log in Hours and Todo. if the expected evidence is missing, review device permission, employee eligibility, and the saved mobile rule before retrying.
