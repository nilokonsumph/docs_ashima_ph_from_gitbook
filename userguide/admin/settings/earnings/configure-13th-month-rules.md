# configure 13th Month rules

## 13th Month

Use this page to review the company rules that the separate 13th Month payroll process uses. These rules help determine employee entitlement and which qualifying pay items enter the calculation.

**Navigation:** Go to **Settings → Paycheck → Earnings → 13th Month**.

## what do these rules control?

Review the available entitlement, inclusion, and calculation choices before starting or recalculating a 13th Month run. The choices shown can depend on your company setup. A company rule is different from an employee-specific exclusion or adjustment made in a particular run.

The dedicated 13th Month process uses the saved rules together with the selected dates, employee entitlement, qualifying payroll values, and supported adjustments. Review their combined effect in the employee detail of a draft run; the Settings screen alone does not show the employee's final result.

## how do i change a rule safely?

1. Open **Settings → Paycheck → Earnings → 13th Month**.
2. Review the saved choices and the pay items or employee groups each choice affects.
3. Complete any conditional fields that appear for the selected option, then save.
4. Recalculate a controlled 13th Month draft and compare the affected employee details, qualifying earnings and deductions, and final totals.
5. If the result is wrong, restore the intended rule or correct the owning employee, payroll, or adjustment record, then recalculate before approval.

The result is reviewed in the 13th Month run, its outputs and downloads, and the dedicated 13th Month payslip. Changing a setting does not itself assign a recurring earning or prove that a prior run has been recalculated.
