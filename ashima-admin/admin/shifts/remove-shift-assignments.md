# Remove shift assignments

use the shift overview to remove an employee's dated assignment when the schedule should no longer apply.

**Important:** removing an assignment may leave dates without schedule context and may change attendance or payroll results. Review the employee calendar and open payroll before continuing.

**Navigation:**\
go to **Shifts → Shifts Overview**.

### how do i remove an assignment?

1. use the weekly date control to display the covered period.
2. find the employee.
3. select one or more employee rows.
4. select **Delete**.
5. review the warning.
6. confirm the action.

### what happens after removal?

the dated assignment no longer appears in the employee calendar. A payroll-group default may still provide schedule context where the company uses one. Confirm the resulting calendar instead of assuming the employee has no shift.

### what should i check before deleting?

* selected week and affected dates;
* employee and payroll group;
* time logs already recorded for those dates;
* approved overtime, rest-day, holiday, leave, or shift-change requests;
* open payroll covering the assignment; and
* whether another assignment or default schedule supplies the expected shift.

### what if the wrong assignment was removed?

assign the correct shift again for the affected dates, then review the employee calendar and timesheet. Recalculate an open payroll when the corrected schedule changes attendance interpretation or paid hours.

removing a dated assignment is different from archiving the reusable shift definition. Use **Manage Shifts** when the shift itself should no longer be available for future assignment.
