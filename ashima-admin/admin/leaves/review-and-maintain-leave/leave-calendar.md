# Leave calendar

use the leave calendar to review scheduled employee leave by day or month before planning attendance, shifts, or payroll review.

**Navigation:**\
go to **Leaves → Leave Calendar**.

### what does this page display?

the calendar organizes leave activity by month and date so administrators can see when employees are scheduled to be away. The visible result depends on the selected leave type, request status, employee scope, and your access.

### how do i review the calendar?

1. select the month and year.
2. select a leave type or keep **All Leave Types**.
3. apply the filter.
4. open a leave entry when the calendar displays one and you need application details.

### how do i download the calendar?

1. select the month, year, and leave-type filter.
2. open the download control.
3. select **XLS** or **CSV**.

the calendar reflects recorded leave applications and their current status. Check leave history before treating a pending request as approved leave.

### how should i use the calendar with Shifts and Hours?

use the calendar to identify possible schedule or attendance impact, then open the employee's leave history, shift calendar, or timesheet for the governing detail. The leave calendar does not replace the employee's approved application or balance ledger.

### why might a leave entry be missing?

check the month and year, leave-type filter, application status, employee visibility, and whether the application was cancelled or rejected. Confirm the application on the employee's leave history before changing the balance.
