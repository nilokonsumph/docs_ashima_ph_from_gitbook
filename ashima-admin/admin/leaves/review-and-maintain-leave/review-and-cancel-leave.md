# Review and cancel leave

use the employee leave details or approval record to review a request and cancel it when the leave should no longer proceed.

> \[!WARNING] cancelling approved leave changes the employee's leave balance and may change an open payroll. Review the dates, status, and payroll impact before continuing.

**Navigation:**\
go to **Leaves → Leave Balance**, open the employee, then review the leave history.

## what should i review first?

check the employee, leave type, covered dates, duration, attachments, current status, and balance allocation. If the dates are already included in an open pay run, plan to recalculate after the cancellation.

## how do i cancel leave?

1. open the employee's leave history.
2. select the application.
3. click the available cancellation action.
4. review the confirmation message.
5. confirm the cancellation.

## what happens after cancellation?

the application status changes and eligible reserved or approved credits return according to the leave type's rules. If a returned credit has already reached its expiry date, it must not become reusable merely because the application was cancelled.
