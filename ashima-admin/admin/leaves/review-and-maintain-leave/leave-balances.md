# Leave balances

use leave balances to review the leave types assigned to an employee and trace earned, used, available, pending, approved, adjusted, or expired credits.

**Navigation:**\
go to **Leaves → Leave Balance**.

### what does the balance list display?

the list shows employee details, leave type, leave units, earned leaves, available balance, pending approval, accrual date, and **View Transaction**. Search for an employee to open the detailed balance and application history.

### what do the balance terms mean?

* **Earned** or **Credits Added** is the amount credited to the employee.
* **Used** is the amount already recorded as taken.
* **Available** is the amount the employee may still apply, after recorded movements.
* **Pending** or **Applied** is reserved while an application waits for a decision.
* **Approved** is allocated to an approved application.
* **Expired** is no longer available because the applicable expiry rule was applied.
* **Adjustment** is a manual increase or decrease with an audit reason.

the page may use different balance labels for ordinary leave and cto. Open the transaction ledger when the summary alone does not explain the balance.

### how do i review one employee?

1. search for the employee.
2. open the employee's leave details.
3. review the assigned leave types, effective dates, accrual dates, balances, and application history.
4. select **View Transaction** for the leave type you need to trace.

### how do i correct a balance?

Open the assigned leave type from the employee's Leave Balance Details page, then choose the action that matches the correction:

* **Add Earned Leaves** — adds credits that the employee earned or should have received.
* **Add Adjustment Leaves** — applies a positive or negative correction. Enter a clear reason so the change is understandable in the ledger.
* **Add Used Leaves** — records credits already used when that use was not created through the normal application flow.

For CTO, a manual credit or deduction is a correction only. Source-generated CTO should come from the approved-work conversion workflow. When CTO expiry is enabled, the correction can also require the applicable configured or custom expiry date and may target a specific pool.

### what is Leave Balance Details?

Leave Balance Details is the employee-specific workspace. Use it to:

* review every assigned leave or CTO type;
* see effective and accrual dates;
* file leave on behalf of the employee;
* open one leave type for earned, adjustment, or used corrections;
* review and cancel eligible history records;
* download the employee's balance or history; and
* open the transaction ledger for reconciliation.

### can an admin apply for leave for an employee?

Yes. Open the employee from **Leaves → Leave Balance**, then use **Apply Leave** for an eligible assigned type. The page checks the employee schedule, leave rules, overlapping records, holiday or rest-day conditions, and applicable payroll restrictions before submission. Required documents can be uploaded when the leave type requires them.

If the action shows **No Entitlement** or is unavailable, confirm the leave assignment, effective start date, available balance, employee eligibility, and company access before proceeding.

### what should i review after a correction?

1. confirm the new earned, used, pending, and available balances;
2. open **View Transaction** and verify the amount, action, reason, and date;
3. review any pending or approved leave that uses the same balance;
4. check expiry or conversion effects when applicable; and
5. review an affected open payroll when the leave dates can change payroll.
