# Leave, CTO, and maternity benefits

Ashima manages ordinary leave, compensatory time off (CTO), and maternity benefits through related employee and approval records, but they are different workflows. Use the correct workflow before changing an employee record.

**Navigation:**\
go to **Leaves → Leave, CTO, and maternity benefits**.

### what is the difference?

| workflow                        | what creates the entitlement or value                                                                              | what the admin mainly manages                                                                                                                                |
| ------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Leave**                       | a configured leave type, employee assignment, and the applicable accrual or opening balance                        | leave policy, eligibility, balance, applications, approval, cancellation, expiry, conversion, and history                                                    |
| **Compensatory Time Off (CTO)** | eligible approved overtime, rest-day work, or holiday work converted into time-off credits                         | CTO source rules, conversion rate, employee assignment, credit pools, expiry, applications, and ledger tracing                                               |
| **Maternity Benefits**          | an employee maternity application and the applicable benefit, contribution, reimbursement, and payroll information | maternity dates, allocated credits, benefit calculation, approval status, SSS reimbursement details, early return to work, payroll effect, print, and export |

### ordinary leave

Use ordinary leave for configured programs such as vacation or sick leave. A leave type defines the reusable company policy. An employee assignment makes that type available to a specific employee and establishes the opening position and effective date.

Typical flow:

1. configure the leave type in **Settings → Leaves**;
2. assign the leave type to the employee;
3. confirm the effective start and opening balance;
4. file or receive a leave application;
5. complete the configured approval workflow;
6. review the resulting balance, history, and transaction ledger; and
7. apply expiry, carryover, conversion, cancellation, or correction only under the configured rule.

### compensatory time off

CTO is not an ordinary annual leave accrual. It turns eligible approved work into time-off credits. The employee must have the matching CTO leave type assigned, and the approved source row must be eligible for conversion.

Typical flow:

1. enable and configure CTO source types;
2. assign the generated CTO leave type to the employee;
3. approve the overtime, rest-day work, or holiday work;
4. convert the eligible source row into CTO;
5. review the credited pool, expiry, and ledger; and
6. let the employee apply for the available CTO balance.

Use the existing **Compensatory Time Off** guide for the full setup, conversion, pool, expiry, application, cancellation, ledger, and paycheck relationship.

### maternity benefits

Maternity Benefits is not an ordinary leave-balance correction and is not CTO. It has its own employee record and calculation view. Admins review the maternity application, dates, allocated credits, contribution history, computed benefit, salary differential, reimbursement information, approval status, early-return settings, and payroll effect.

Typical flow:

1. confirm the employee maternity application and approval status;
2. open the employee's maternity-benefit details;
3. review the maternity type, dates, allocated credits, expected delivery date, and contribution period;
4. review the computed SSS benefit, salary differential, contributions, and total disbursement;
5. maintain SSS notification and reimbursement information when available;
6. record working-during-leave or early-return information only when applicable;
7. review the affected payroll and recalculate when required; and
8. use the available print or export output after the record is complete.

### which page should i use?

* Use **Leaves → Leave Balance** to assign leave, review balances, file leave for an employee, and open balance details or the ledger.
* Use **Leaves → CTO Settings / CTO Conversions** for CTO setup and approved-work conversion.
* Use **Paycheck → Maternity Benefits** to review and maintain maternity-benefit records and payroll-related results.
* Use **To Do's** to review requests that are waiting for the assigned approval action.

**Important:** \[!NOTE] **Important:** A related page location does not make these workflows interchangeable. Check whether the record is ordinary leave, source-converted CTO, or a maternity benefit before changing balances or dates.
