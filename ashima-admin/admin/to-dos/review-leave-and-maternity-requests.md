# Review leave and maternity requests

use the leave or maternity Todo queue to review dates, eligibility, supporting records, and conflicts before deciding a request.

> \[!WARNING] approval changes attendance and may affect payroll. Confirm the covered dates and employee eligibility before continuing.

**Navigation:**\
go to **Todo → Leave** or **Todo → Maternity Benefits**.

## what should i review?

* employee and request type
* covered date or date range
* requested duration
* assigned leave type and available balance for leave requests
* schedule and overlapping requests
* reason and supporting documents
* maternity details and benefit calculation when applicable

## how do i approve or reject?

1. search for the employee or request.
2. open or load the request details.
3. review the dates, balance, schedule, and documents.
4. click **Approve** or **Reject**.
5. enter the required note or reason.
6. confirm the decision.

## what happens after the decision?

approved leave updates the employee's leave history and balance and is considered by attendance and payroll. Rejected leave releases any reserved credits. Approved maternity follows the maternity-benefit and attendance rules rather than an ordinary leave balance.
