# Review overtime, rest day, and holiday requests

use the premium-work Todo queues to confirm captured time, schedule context, and eligibility before approving overtime, rest-day, or holiday work.

> \[!WARNING] approved premium work may be paid in payroll or converted to cto when allowed. Confirm the source work has not already been used for another payment or conversion.

**Navigation:**\
go to **Todo → Overtime** or **Todo → Timesheet**, then select the applicable rest-day or holiday type.

## what should i review?

* employee and work date
* assigned shift and scheduled rest day
* holiday classification when applicable
* captured clock-in and clock-out
* eligible minutes or hours
* existing application, approval, payment, or cto conversion

## how do i approve or reject?

1. open the applicable queue.
2. search for the employee.
3. load the source time and schedule details.
4. review the eligible amount and duplicate-use risk.
5. click **Approve** or **Reject**.
6. review the number of selected items in the confirmation.
7. optionally select **Download a copy** when you need a record before deciding.
8. confirm the decision.

the reviewed Overtime approval confirmation did not ask for a separate note. Follow any additional field shown for the request type you are reviewing.

## what happens after the decision?

approved work becomes available to the applicable payroll or cto process. Rejected work remains captured as time but does not become payable under the rejected application.
