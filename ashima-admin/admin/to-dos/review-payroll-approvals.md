# Review payroll approvals

use the payroll Todo queue to review a submitted pay run and its supporting outputs before approving or rejecting payroll.

> \[!WARNING] payroll approval finalizes a critical stage of processing and may lock recalculation. Resolve warnings and source updates before approval.

**Navigation:**\
go to **Todo → Payroll**.

## what should i review?

* company, payroll group, coverage period, and pay date
* employee count, excluded employees, and total net pay
* recalculation or source-update warnings
* payroll masterlist and bank file
* material employee pay details and ingestion entries
* approval notes and prior rejection reason

## how do i decide a payroll request?

1. open the submitted pay run.
2. review the payroll summary and warnings.
3. open the payroll masterlist and material employee details.
4. confirm the bank-file total and employee coverage.
5. click the available approval or rejection action.
6. enter the required note.
7. confirm the decision.

## what happens after the decision?

approval moves payroll to its next controlled status. Rejection returns it for correction, where the payroll team can update sources and recalculate before submitting again.
