# Review disbursement approvals

use the Disbursement Todo queue to review a submitted employee payment batch before approving or rejecting it.

**Important:** disbursement approval can make bank, cash, and payslip outputs available after closing. Confirm the employees, totals, tax classification, and recalculation time before deciding.

**Navigation:**\
go to **Todo → Disbursement**.

### what should i review?

* disbursement date and voucher number;
* employee scope and confidentiality;
* amount per employee and total amount;
* taxable and non-taxable classification;
* payroll-inclusion and tax method where applicable;
* latest recalculation time; and
* supporting business reason or reference.

### how do i decide the request?

1. open the Disbursement queue.
2. open the submitted record.
3. compare the header total with the employee rows.
4. review material or unusual employee amounts.
5. confirm that recalculation is current.
6. select the approval or rejection action available to you.
7. enter a reason when requested and confirm the decision.

### what happens after approval?

the configured workflow advances. When the final approval closes the disbursement, the owning Disbursement page can provide the applicable bank, cash, report, and payslip actions.

### what happens after rejection?

the record shows a rejected result. The preparer must review the reason, correct the eligible draft or source information, recalculate, and submit again when appropriate. Rejection does not create a replacement payment automatically.
