# Review 13th month, year-end, and termination approvals

use the special-payroll Todo queues to review 13th month, year-end, or termination outputs before completing their approval step.

**Important:** these approvals affect employee tax, final pay, statutory documents, or disbursement. Resolve recalculation warnings and employee exceptions before approval.

**Navigation:**\
go to **Todo**, then open **13th Month**, **Year End**, or **Termination**.

### what should i review?

* selected year, period, or termination date
* included and excluded employees
* recalculation status
* employee detail and adjustment breakdown
* tax, contribution, and ingestion values where applicable
* masterlist, bank file, statutory output, or final-pay report

### how do i decide the request?

1. open the applicable queue.
2. select the run or employee.
3. review the summary and material employee details.
4. confirm the available downloads and totals.
5. click the available approval or rejection action.
6. enter the required reason.
7. confirm the decision.

### what happens after the decision?

the result follows the action shown for that queue. An approval advances or completes the applicable workflow. A rejection or returned item must be corrected in its source module and recalculated before it is submitted again.

do not assume every special-payroll queue provides the same actions. If the page presents information without an approval or rejection control, use it as a review surface and complete the next action from the owning 13th Month, Year End, or Termination page.
