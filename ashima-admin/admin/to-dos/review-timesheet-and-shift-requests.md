# Review timesheet and shift requests

use the timesheet, shift, open-shift, or mobile clock-in Todo queue to review requested time and schedule changes before they affect attendance.

> \[!WARNING] an approved time or shift correction may change attendance and open payroll. Review both the original and requested values before deciding.

**Navigation:**\
go to **Todo**, then open **Timesheet**, **Shifts**, **Open Shift**, or **Mobile Clock-In**.

## what should i review?

* employee and covered date
* original and requested time or shift
* assigned schedule
* reason and supporting evidence
* overlapping leave, overtime, rest-day, or holiday request
* open-payroll impact

## how do i approve or reject?

1. open the correct queue.
2. search for the employee or request.
3. open the supporting details.
4. compare the original and requested record.
5. click **Approve** or **Reject**.
6. review the number of selected items in the confirmation.
7. optionally select **Download a copy** when you need a record before deciding.
8. confirm the decision.

the reviewed Timesheet rejection confirmation did not ask for a separate reason. Follow any additional field shown for the request type you are reviewing.

## what happens after the decision?

approval updates the applicable time or schedule source. Rejection keeps the prior accepted source and records the decision. Recalculate an affected open pay run.

if a pending timesheet item belongs to a closed payroll, Ashima blocks approval. The queue provides a separate action to reject closed-payroll items so they can be removed from the pending list. Review the affected items before using the bulk action.
