# Disbursement

Use Disbursement to prepare and release payments that are processed separately from a regular pay run.

Disbursement gives payroll administrators a controlled workflow for selecting employees, entering payment items, reviewing tax treatment, recalculating results, obtaining approval, and producing payment files.

**Important:** Before creating a disbursement, confirm that the payment should not be included in a regular pay run. Review the taxable or non-taxable classification carefully because it affects payroll and year-end reporting.

**navigation:**\
Go to **Utilities → Disbursement**.

### what can you do from the disbursement list?

The page separates **Draft Runs** from **History**. Depending on the record's status, you can:

* open a disbursement for review
* create a new disbursement with **Disburse**
* continue a draft
* remove selected drafts
* import multiple entries
* review approved or closed records

### what is the usual disbursement flow?

1. Create a disbursement or import entries.
2. Select the employees and payment classifications.
3. Save the record as a draft.
4. Open the preview and verify each amount.
5. Recalculate the disbursement.
6. Submit it for approval.
7. Complete the approval workflow.
8. Produce the required payment and report files.

### why are drafts separate?

A draft is still editable and has not entered the approval workflow. Removing a draft removes unfinished work; it does not cancel an approved payment.

### what happens after approval?

The approved disbursement remains available for review. Its approved taxable and non-taxable amounts can be used by related payroll, employee record, termination, and year-end processes.
