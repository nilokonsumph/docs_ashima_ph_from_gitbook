# Birthdays and anniversaries

Use the Birthdays and Anniversaries pages to review employees celebrating during the current month.

**navigation:**\
Go to **Dashboard**, then open **Birthdays** or **Anniversaries** from the applicable dashboard card.

## what does the birthdays page show?

The page lists the employee, position, and date of birth for employees with birthdays in the current month.

## what does the anniversaries page show?

The page lists the employee, position, and date hired for employees with work anniversaries in the current month.

## what should you check when an employee is missing?

1. Confirm the employee is active.
2. Check the employee's date of birth or date hired in Workforce.
3. Confirm that you have permission to view the employee.
4. Check whether confidential-employee access limits the result.
5. Return to the page after correcting the source record.

## what happens after employee details are updated?

The birthday or anniversary list uses the employee's saved Workforce information. Correct the employee record if a date is wrong; do not attempt to edit it from the dashboard list.
