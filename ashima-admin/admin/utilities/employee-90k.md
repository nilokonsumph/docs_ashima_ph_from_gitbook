# Employee 90k

use employee 90k to review how much of the annual non-taxable 90,000 limit each employee has used, what remains, and any excess above the limit.

**Navigation:**\
go to **Reports → Tax → 90k**.

## what does the summary display?

the summary lists employees for the selected year with their 90k consumption, remaining balance, and excess. Open an employee to review the year-specific ledger.

## what counts toward the limit?

eligible non-taxable other earnings and exempt allowances from payroll consume the employee's 90k limit. The employee ledger keeps the original eligible amounts traceable even when the annual total exceeds 90,000.

## how do i review one employee?

1. select the year.
2. search for the employee; use the table headings to review or sort employee, department, payroll group, basic pay, total 90k, available 90k, and excess 90k values.
3. open the employee detail.
4. review each ledger entry and running balance.
5. compare an unexpected row with the related approved payroll or supported payout.

## how do i download the report?

use **Download** on the summary or employee detail and select the available output. Choose the detailed output when you need source-level entries.

## what does recalculate do?

recalculation rebuilds the employee 90k snapshot from eligible source records. This action is support-controlled on the current page; use it only when the displayed balance needs an authorized refresh.
