# WTT adjustments

use the WTT tool to review withholding-tax-to-date values, recalculate the stored year position, and maintain available employee or 13th-month adjustments.

**Important:** withholding tax adjustments affect payroll tax. Review the employee, year, and open payroll before saving or recalculating.

**Navigation:**\
go to **Reports → Tax → Wtt Tool**.

### what does this page display?

the page lists employee, payroll group, current and projected taxable values, current tax withheld, projected tax due, projected 13th month, tax gap, tax-gap adjustment, recommended adjustment, and actions. It provides **Bulk WTT Adjustments**, **Adjust 13th Month**, **Recalculate All**, search, and filters.

### how do i refresh WTT data?

1. review whether updates are available.
2. select the employee scope when required.
3. start recalculation.
4. wait for the recalculation to complete.
5. review the updated values.

### how do adjustments work?

the employee plus adjustment changes the amount included in the recommended tax-gap calculation. The 13th-month adjustment changes the projected 13th-month assumption used by the tool.

an adjustment changes the projection input; it does not by itself prove that tax was deducted in payroll. Review the affected open payroll and its employee tax detail after applying or releasing an adjustment.

### what should i review before accepting an adjustment?

* selected tax year and employee;
* current taxable pay and tax withheld;
* projected taxable pay and projected 13th Month;
* tax gap and recommended adjustment;
* prior adjustments already released or reverted; and
* the open payroll where the adjustment is expected to apply.

### what if the projection is incorrect?

check compensation, taxable earnings, prior-employer values, payroll status, 13th Month assumptions, and existing adjustments. Correct the source when possible, recalculate the projection, and review the result again. Use a direct adjustment only for an approved correction or planned tax treatment.

### what happens after release or reversion?

released adjustments become available to the supported payroll flow. Reversion removes the released adjustment from future use but does not rewrite a payroll that has already been closed. Review the adjustment history and the affected payroll after either action.
