# Punctuality pages

use the punctuality pages to review employees who arrived before their shift or recorded tardiness against their assigned schedule.

**Navigation:**\
go to **Hours → Punctuality**, then select **Early Birds** or **Tardy**.

## what do these pages display?

* **Early Birds** shows time entries recorded before the employee's expected shift start.
* **Tardy** shows the delay between the employee's expected start and qualifying time entry.

the assigned shift gives the time entry its meaning. A clock-in time without the correct shift may produce a misleading punctuality result.

## how do i review punctuality?

1. open **Early Birds** or **Tardy**.
2. select the date using the page's calendar control.
3. search for an employee and apply the available work schedule, department, or position filters.
4. review the scheduled time, recorded time, and minutes shown.
5. open the employee's timesheet when a row needs correction or explanation.

## how does tardiness affect payroll?

tardiness affects payroll only when the company's deduction rules use tardy minutes. Correct the timesheet and confirm the assigned shift before recalculating an open pay run.

## how do i download the list?

apply the filters, open the download control, and select **Excel** or **CSV**.
