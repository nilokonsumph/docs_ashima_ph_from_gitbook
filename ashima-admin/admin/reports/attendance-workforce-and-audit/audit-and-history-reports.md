# Audit and history reports

Use Audit Trail and history reports to trace saved actions and record changes in Ashima.

Audit information supports investigation, but it should be read together with the affected employee, payroll, shift, or approval record.

**navigation:**\
go to **Reports → Others → Audit Trail** or **Log Summary**. Use **Reports → Pay Run → Compensation History** or **Reports → Shifts → Shift History** for those focused histories.

## what can you review?

Audit Trail lists date/time, user, module, category, change from, and change to. It provides date, module, employee, category, and user filters. Open the export menu to choose **XLS** or **CSV**. Separate reports exist for compensation and shift changes.

## how do you investigate a change?

1. Select the relevant date range.
2. Narrow the list using available filters.
3. Locate the affected employee or record.
4. Compare the audit entry with the source page and approval history.
5. Confirm whether a later correction or recalculation followed the change.
6. Download the audit output when it is needed for review.

## why might the audit trail not explain the payroll amount by itself?

The audit trail records activity. Payroll calculations can also depend on shifts, time logs, approvals, compensation, settings, and recalculation timing. Use the related report or detail page to understand the final amount.
