# Workforce reports

Use Workforce reports to review employee lists and headcount. Compensation History is under Pay Run reports, while Shift History is under Shifts reports.

**navigation:**\
Go to **Reports**, then select the required Workforce report.

### which workforce reports are available?

* Employee List
* Headcount
* Compensation History
* Shift History

**Employee List** and **Headcount** describe the workforce population for the selected scope. **Compensation History** explains effective-dated pay changes. **Shift History** explains schedule changes. Choose the report that owns the question instead of treating all four as interchangeable employee lists.

### how do you use a workforce report?

1. Select the required report.
2. Choose the period, employee scope, or organizational filters shown.
3. Generate or save the report.
4. Open the detail or list.
5. Check employee status, organization, and dates.
6. Download the output when needed.

### why can employee counts differ between reports?

Reports can use different dates, statuses, or employee scopes. Confidential access and manager visibility can also limit which employees you see.

check whether the report is effective as of one date or covers a range, whether inactive or separated employees are included, and whether organization filters such as payroll group, department, location, or employment status are active.

### where should corrections be made?

Correct employee details in Workforce, compensation changes in Compensation Details, and shift records in Shifts. Regenerate the affected report afterward.

do not edit a downloaded file as the only correction. That file will diverge from Ashima and the next generated report will still use the unchanged source record.
