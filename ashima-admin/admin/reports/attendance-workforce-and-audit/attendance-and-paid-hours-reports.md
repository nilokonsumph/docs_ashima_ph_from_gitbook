# Attendance and paid hours reports

Use attendance reports to compare timekeeping records, scheduled work, and paid payroll hours.

**navigation:**\
go to **Reports → Hours**, then select **Attendance** or **Paid Hours**. **Log Summary** is available under **Reports → Others**.

## what is the attendance report for?

The Attendance Report summarizes attendance information for the selected scope and provides a saved detail and download flow.

## what is log summary for?

Log Summary presents employee time-log information for the selected period. Use it when you need a focused record of captured punches and related time data.

## what is paid hours reconciliation for?

Paid Hours Reconciliation helps compare paid payroll hours with timekeeping information. It supports report creation, report detail, employee-scope review, and download.

## how do you investigate a difference?

1. Confirm the report period and employee scope.
2. Check the employee's saved time logs.
3. Check the shift assigned for each affected date.
4. Review approved time, overtime, rest-day, holiday, and leave requests.
5. Compare the result with the calculated pay run.
6. Correct the source record when needed.
7. Recalculate payroll and regenerate the report.
