# SSS reports

Use SSS reports to review employee SSS contribution, loan, and registration information prepared from Ashima records.

**Important:** Confirm employee SSS numbers, contribution settings, payroll periods, and finalized payroll amounts before using a report for submission or payment.

**navigation:**\
Go to **Reports**, then open the required **SSS** report.

### which SSS reports are available?

* **R3:** employee contribution information for the selected reporting period.
* **R5:** contribution payment information.
* **ML1:** member loan payment information.
* **R1A:** employee registration or employment information supported by the page.

### how do you prepare an SSS report?

1. Select the required SSS report.
2. Enter the reporting month or period.
3. Complete the required employer or payment fields.
4. Save or generate the report.
5. Open the detail or preview.
6. Review employee SSS numbers and amounts.
7. Compare totals with the related payroll results.
8. Download or print the available output.

### why might an employee be missing?

Check the employee's SSS entitlement, SSS number, employment status, payroll inclusion, contribution result, and your permission to view the employee.

also confirm that the selected reporting period contains the payroll contribution or loan deduction and that the supplying payroll has reached the status required by the report. Correct the employee, contribution, loan, or payroll source before regenerating the output.

### does an Ashima output confirm submission or payment?

no. The report prepares information from Ashima records. Review the file and complete the required submission or payment through the applicable external process.
