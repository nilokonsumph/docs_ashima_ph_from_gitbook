# Statutory reports

use this section for SSS, PhilHealth, HDMF, BIR, and withholding-tax reports prepared from employee and payroll records.

**Navigation:**\
go to **Reports → Statutory reports**.

### before generating

confirm the reporting period, employee government numbers, entitlement, employment status, payroll inclusion, contribution or tax result, and required employer information.

select the report required for the filing or remittance purpose. Correct employee or payroll information at its source and regenerate the output. An Ashima download prepares information; it does not independently confirm external filing, acceptance, or payment.
