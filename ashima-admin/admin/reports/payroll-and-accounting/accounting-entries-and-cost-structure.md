# Accounting entries and cost structure

Use Accounting Entries to prepare payroll journal information and Cost Structure to review or allocate payroll costs.

**navigation:**\
go to **Reports → Pay Run**, then select **Accounting Entries** or **Cost Structure**.

## what are accounting entries for?

Accounting Entries organize payroll amounts for journal review. The report is based on the selected payroll data and the company's payroll accounting setup.

## how do you generate accounting entries?

1. Open Accounting Entries.
2. Select the applicable pay run or report period.
3. Save the report.
4. Open the detail page.
5. Review totals and account presentation.
6. Download the report for accounting review.

## what is cost structure for?

Cost Structure groups payroll costs and can provide employee-level allocations. The released page supports generating employees, reviewing a report detail, opening an employee allocation, updating it, and downloading the result.

## how do you review cost structure?

1. Open Cost Structure.
2. Select the payroll data to include.
3. Save the report.
4. Generate or review its employee list.
5. Open an employee when allocation review is needed.
6. Update the allocation only when authorized.
7. Download the completed report.
