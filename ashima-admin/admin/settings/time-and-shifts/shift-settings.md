# Shift settings

use shift settings to create and maintain the reusable schedules assigned to employees.

**Navigation:**\
go to **Settings → Shifts**.

## what shift types are available?

* uniform shifts use defined start, break, and end times.
* flexible shifts allow work within configured flexible rules.
* open shifts calculate work without a fixed daily start and end in the same way as a regular uniform schedule.
* rest-day and night-shift settings provide related schedule or premium rules.

## how do i create or update a shift?

1. open the required shift type.
2. click the add action or open an existing shift.
3. enter the schedule name and required work rules.
4. complete the daily work and rest-day information.
5. review grace, break, and related options.
6. click **Save**.

## what happens after saving?

the shift becomes available for payroll-group defaults and employee assignments. Editing a shift used by employees changes their schedule interpretation; use **Save As New** when a different rule should coexist with the original.
