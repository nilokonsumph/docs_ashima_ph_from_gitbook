# Overtime, tardiness, and break settings

use hours settings to define how Ashima identifies eligible overtime, tardiness, undertime, grace periods, and break-related deficiencies.

> \[!WARNING] these rules affect attendance and payroll calculations. Review an open payroll and recalculate after changing a rule used by its employees.

**Navigation:**\
go to **Settings → Hours** and open the applicable setting.

## what do these settings affect?

* overtime settings determine when post-shift work becomes eligible and whether an application is required.
* tardiness settings define grace and deduction behavior.
* break settings define expected break time and overbreak treatment.
* related employee entitlements determine whether the calculated item applies to a specific employee.

## how do i update a rule?

1. open the applicable hours setting.
2. review the main enablement option.
3. update the available calculation or approval choices.
4. review employee-level assignments or entitlements that depend on it.
5. save the change.

## what happens after saving?

newly interpreted timesheets and recalculated open payrolls use the updated rule. The system still needs a valid shift and time entries to determine the applicable minutes.
