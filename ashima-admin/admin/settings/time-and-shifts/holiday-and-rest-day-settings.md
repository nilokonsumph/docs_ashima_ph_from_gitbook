# Holiday and rest day settings

use holiday and rest day settings to define whether employees must apply for work on those dates and how approved work becomes eligible for payroll.

> \[!WARNING] changing an application or pay rule can change open payroll results for affected dates. Recalculate before payroll approval.

**Navigation:**\
go to **Settings → Shifts**, then open **Holiday Pay** or **Rest Day**.

## what should i configure?

* whether holiday-related pay requires workflow approval
* the applicable approval workflow
* the available holiday or rest-day pay rule
* employee entitlement and any related premium setting

## how do these settings affect timesheets?

Ashima captures qualifying time entries even when the date is a rest day or holiday. When approval is required, corresponding holiday pay remains subject to workflow approval.

when one date is both a rest day and holiday, the page should offer one applicable action rather than duplicate applications. Holiday leads when both applications are required under the approved product rule.
