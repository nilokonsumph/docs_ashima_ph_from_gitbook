# Settings overview

use Settings to define the company rules and reusable options that Workforce, Hours, Shifts, Leaves, Paycheck, and approvals use.

**Navigation:**\
open **Settings**, then select the relevant settings family. Settings is a navigation menu; there is no single settings landing page.

## how are settings organized?

* company and workforce settings define the organization structure and employee choices.
* hours and shift settings define how schedules and time entries are interpreted.
* paycheck settings define payroll groups, schedules, pay items, deductions, contributions, tax, and payslip behavior.
* leave settings define eligibility, accrual, expiry, conversion, and approval-related rules.
* workflow and manager-access settings define approval routing and employee visibility.
* imports and upload manager provide controlled bulk entry using Ashima templates.

## what should i check before changing a setting?

identify whether the setting affects all employees, a payroll group, or an employee assignment. Review open payrolls before changing a rule that affects pay, time, leave, or contributions.

> \[!WARNING] a payroll-critical setting change may require recalculation of an open pay run. Complete changes before payroll approval whenever possible.
