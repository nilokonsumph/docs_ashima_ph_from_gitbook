# Company and workforce

use this section to configure company structure and workforce reference information used by employees, access, payroll, and reports.

**Navigation:**\
go to **Settings → Company and workforce**.

review locations, departments, cost centers, positions, employment types, ranks, company registration, and related workforce setup before assigning them to employees. A reference value can affect filtering, reporting, payroll scope, and manager visibility.

do not delete or replace an in-use value without reviewing affected employees and records. Correct employee assignments after a structural change.
