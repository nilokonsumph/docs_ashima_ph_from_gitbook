# Company structure

use company and workforce settings to maintain the organization values selected in employee records, payroll allocation, approvals, and reports.

**Navigation:**\
go to **Settings → Workforce**.

## what can i maintain?

* **Company Profile** — company identity, contact, and branding information.
* **Location / Worksite** — office or work locations used by employees and reports.
* **Government Registration** — company registration values used by statutory outputs.
* **Departments** — organization units used for employee assignment, filtering, visibility, and approvals.
* **Cost Center** — payroll-cost allocation choices.
* **Project** — project assignment and reporting choices.
* **Job Position** — employee role choices.
* **Employment Type** — employment classification choices.
* **Rank** — employee rank choices where the company uses rank-based rules.

## how do i add or update an item?

1. open the relevant settings page.
2. click the add action or open an existing item.
3. enter or update the required fields. For a department, enter the **Department Name** and optional **Description**.
4. review whether employees or reports already use the item.
5. click **Save**.

> \[!WARNING] deleting an item already used by employees may affect records and reports. A controlled test confirmed deletion only for an unused disposable department; protection for in-use departments still requires confirmation. Use an inactive or replacement approach when the page supports it.

## what happens after saving?

the item becomes available in the related employee, payroll, approval, or report filters. Updating a shared item changes how the same reference appears wherever it is used.

On the Departments page, the list shows who created and last updated each department and the corresponding dates. To remove a department, select its row, click **Delete**, and confirm **Yes**. Only delete a department after confirming that it is not needed by employee or related records.
