# Workflow and manager access

use workflow and manager-access settings to control who reviews requests and which employees a manager may see.

> \[!WARNING] changing workflow or visibility settings can reroute approvals or expose employee records. Review the affected managers and pending requests before saving.

**Navigation:**\
go to **Settings → Workflow** or **Manager Privileges**.

## what is the difference between workflow and visibility?

workflow settings define approval routing for requests such as leave, overtime, timesheet changes, shifts, and payroll. Manager visibility defines which employees the manager can access in supported modules.

page privileges remain separate. A manager or administrator still needs access to the module in addition to the correct employee scope.

## how do i update a workflow or manager scope?

1. open the applicable setting.
2. select the workflow group, manager, or access scope.
3. review the employees or departments included.
4. update the approvers or visibility choices.
5. review the effect on pending requests.
6. click **Save**.

## what happens after saving?

new requests and supported pages use the updated routing or visibility. Existing pending requests may retain or recalculate their routing according to the specific workflow rule; confirm this during live validation before publishing a broader promise.
