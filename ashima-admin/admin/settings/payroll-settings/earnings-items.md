# Earnings items

Use Earnings settings to create the reusable allowance, other-earning, hazard-pay, and related earning definitions available for employee assignment.

**Navigation:**\
go to **Settings → Paycheck → Earnings**, then select the required earning family.

## before you create or change an item

Confirm:

* the business purpose and earning family;
* whether it is taxable or non-taxable under the approved payroll setup;
* whether it is recurring or one-time;
* the supported schedule, amount, or calculation method;
* whether the item contributes to other payroll calculations or reports; and
* whether employees or historical payroll already use it.

> \[!WARNING] When the business or tax meaning changes materially, create a new item. Changing an established item can alter the meaning of current and historical payroll records.

## which earning family should i use?

| family                 | intended use                                                         | employee-level review                                                  |
| ---------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| allowance              | a reusable allowance assigned to eligible employees                  | amount, dates, recurrence, and employee assignment                     |
| other earnings         | another supported earning that is not ordinary basic pay             | earning purpose, one-time or recurring behavior, and payroll treatment |
| hazard pay             | hazard-related pay under the applicable company rule                 | employee eligibility, amount or rate, and effective dates              |
| compensation or salary | the employee's base compensation record                              | pay rate, effective date, payroll group, and salary history            |
| retroactive pay        | a separate result produced from an effective-dated salary correction | affected periods, generated amount, review, and recalculation          |

Do not use one family merely because its visible fields allow the amount. Choose the family that matches the business and reporting meaning.

## how do i create an earning item?

1. Open the required earning family.
2. Select the add action.
3. Enter the name and required classification.
4. Complete the displayed tax, recurrence, schedule, amount, or calculation fields.
5. Review the business meaning and downstream use.
6. Save the item.

## what happens after saving?

The item becomes available for supported employee assignment. It does not automatically affect every employee.

After assigning or changing an item, review:

* the employee assignment and effective date;
* affected open payroll;
* recalculation state;
* payslip presentation; and
* payroll and tax reports.

## how should i handle an item already in use?

Prefer an inactive, end-dated, or replacement approach when supported. Do not delete or repurpose an item solely to prevent future use if it is already referenced by payroll history.
