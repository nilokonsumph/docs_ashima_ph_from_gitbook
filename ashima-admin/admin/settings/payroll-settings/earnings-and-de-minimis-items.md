# Earnings and de minimis items

use earnings settings to create the reusable allowance, other earning, hazard pay, and de minimis items that may be assigned to employees.

> \[!WARNING] changing the tax or calculation nature of an item already in use can change payroll and tax reporting. Create a new item when the business meaning changes materially.

**Navigation:**\
go to **Settings → Paycheck → Earnings**, then select the item family.

## what is the difference between setup and assignment?

the settings page defines what the item is and how it behaves. The employee assignment selects the item and supplies applicable employee values, dates, or schedules.

## how do i create an item?

1. open the allowance, de minimis, hazard pay, or other earnings page.
2. click the add action.
3. enter the name and required classification.
4. complete the tax, recurrence, schedule, or calculation fields shown for that family.
5. review the item before saving.
6. click **Save**.

## what happens after saving?

the item becomes available for employee assignment and related payroll use. Mark an item inactive or stop new assignment when the page supports it; do not repurpose an established item for a different payroll meaning.
