# De minimis settings

Use De Minimis settings to define the company items that can be assigned to employees under the supported de minimis workflow.

**Navigation:**\
go to **Settings → Paycheck → De Minimis**.

## what does this page control?

The settings page defines the company item and the fields shown for its current classification. The employee De Minimis page assigns that item to an employee and supplies supported employee-specific values.

An updated statutory threshold does not automatically prove that every existing employee assignment should be changed. Review the company item, assignment, effective date, and current payroll treatment.

## before you create or update an item

Confirm:

* the approved item name and business purpose;
* the current classification and available threshold or limit;
* the effective date for new use;
* whether employees already use the item; and
* whether an open payroll already contains it.

> \[!WARNING] Do not change the business or tax meaning of an item already used in historical payroll. Create a replacement item when the meaning changes materially.

## how do i create or update an item?

1. Open De Minimis settings.
2. Select the add action or open an existing eligible item.
3. Complete the displayed name, classification, limit, and other required fields.
4. Review whether the item is already assigned.
5. Save the item.
6. Assign it to eligible employees through the employee De Minimis workflow.

## what should i check after a change?

* the correct item is available for assignment;
* existing assignments retain their intended meaning;
* new assignments use the correct effective date and amount;
* affected open payroll is recalculated; and
* payslip and payroll reports show the intended treatment.
