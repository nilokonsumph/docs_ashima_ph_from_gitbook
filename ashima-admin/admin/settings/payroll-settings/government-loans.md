# Government loans

Use Government Loans to define supported government loan types and assign an employee's loan, remittance amount, payment schedule, and coverage dates.

**Navigation:**\
for setup, go to **Settings → Paycheck → Deductions → Government Loans**.\
for assignment, go to **Workforce → Government Loans**.

## which loan types are supported?

The current settings page offers these government-loan types:

* HDMF Loan - MPL;
* HDMF Calamity Loan;
* SSS Salary Loan;
* SSS Calamity Loan; and
* SSS Emergency Loan.

Use Third-Party Loans for another lender or company-supported non-government loan.

## how do i make a government loan type available?

1. Open **Settings → Paycheck → Deductions → Government Loans**.
2. Select **Add Government Loans**.
3. Select the loan type.
4. Select **Applicable For**:
   * All;
   * Employment Type;
   * Rank; or
   * Department.
5. Complete the displayed group selection.
6. Save the loan type.

Creating the type does not create an employee loan or deduct an amount.

## how do i assign a government loan?

1. Open **Workforce → Government Loans**.
2. Select the assign action.
3. Search for and select the employee.
4. Enter the loan number.
5. Select the available government loan type.
6. Enter **Remittance Due**.
7. Select the payment schedule.
8. Enter **Date From** and **Date To** using supported employee payroll dates.
9. Review the employee pay schedule displayed by the page.
10. Save the loan.
11. Recalculate and review the affected open payroll.

For a semi-monthly employee, the selected remittance dates and payment schedule must align with the employee's payroll calendar.

## how do i manage or stop a government loan?

* Review the loan's current status, dates, and remittance due before changing it.
* Use the supported end date, completion, pause, or delete action available for the record.
* Do not create another active loan record merely to correct a loan number, date, or remittance amount when the current record is editable.
* Do not delete a historical loan assignment to reverse a completed payroll deduction.

## downloads and review

Use the available list filters and download action to review government-loan assignments. Confirm the employee, loan type, remittance due, payment schedule, start/end dates, and status.

## warnings and troubleshooting

* **No active loan type is available:** add or activate the applicable government loan type and confirm employee applicability.
* **Date is not accepted:** use a date allowed by the employee's payroll calendar.
* **Loan missing from payroll:** review status, date range, payment schedule, employee payroll group, and recalculation.
* **Possible duplicate:** compare loan number, type, employee, dates, and existing active records before assigning another loan.
* Completed payroll is historical; correct it through the approved payroll-adjustment process rather than deleting the current loan record.
