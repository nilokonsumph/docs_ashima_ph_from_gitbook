# Deductions and loans settings

use deduction settings to create reusable government-loan, third-party-loan, insurance, and other-deduction options for employee assignment.

> \[!WARNING] changing an assigned deduction definition can affect employee balances and payroll. Create a new item when its business meaning or core rule changes.

**Navigation:**\
go to **Settings → Paycheck → Deductions**, then select the deduction family.

## what does the settings page define?

the settings page defines the item name, family, and available calculation or schedule rules. The employee page records who receives the deduction and the employee-specific amount, balance, dates, or amortization.

## how do i create or update a deduction option?

1. open the required deduction family.
2. click the add action or open an existing item.
3. complete the required fields.
4. review whether employees already use the item.
5. click **Save**.

## what happens after saving?

the item becomes available for employee assignment. An item definition alone does not deduct an amount from every employee.
