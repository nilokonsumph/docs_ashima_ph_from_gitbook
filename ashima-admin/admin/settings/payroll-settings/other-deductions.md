# Other deductions

Use Other Deductions for an approved employee deduction that is not a government loan, third-party loan, insurance deduction, or statutory contribution.

**Navigation:**\
for setup, go to **Settings → Paycheck → Deductions → Other Deductions**.\
for assignment, go to **Deductions → Other Deductions**, then open the employee.

## before you begin

* confirm the deduction name, description, and legal or business basis;
* confirm the approved deduction amount;
* identify eligible employees;
* decide whether it is one-time or recurring;
* for recurring deductions, choose interval or occurrence behavior; and
* review whether an item with the same name or purpose already exists.

Active deduction names must be unique.

## recurrence choices

| choice                 | use it when                 | schedule information                                           |
| ---------------------- | --------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| non-recurring          | deduct once                 | pay date                                                       |
| recurring — interval   | deduct between stated dates | deduction schedule, payroll schedule, start date, and end date |
| recurring — occurrence | deduct for a fixed count    | number of occurrences, payroll schedule, and start date        |

## how do i create an other-deduction item?

1. Open **Settings → Paycheck → Deductions → Other Deductions**.
2. Select the add action.
3. Enter a unique deduction name and description.
4. Select **Applicable For**: all employees, rank, department, or employment type.
5. Enter the deduction amount.
6. Choose whether the item is recurring.
7. For a non-recurring item, select the pay date.
8. For a recurring item, select **Interval** or **Occurrence** and complete the displayed schedule fields.
9. Review the item, then save.

## how do i assign it to an employee?

1. Open **Deductions → Other Deductions**.
2. Search for the employee and open the employee detail.
3. Select the assign action.
4. Select the deduction item.
5. Confirm the amount, recurrence, dates, and payroll schedule.
6. Save the assignment.
7. Confirm the new row and its status.
8. Recalculate and review the affected open payroll.

## how do i stop, complete, correct, or delete it?

* Edit an eligible record to correct amount or schedule when its business meaning remains unchanged.
* Let a fixed occurrence count or end date complete the deduction when that matches the obligation.
* Use the supported inactive, completed, or delete action only after reviewing open payroll and historical use.
* Do not reuse an old deduction name for a different obligation.

Deleting a current assignment does not reverse a completed payroll. Use the approved payroll-adjustment process when a completed deduction must be corrected.

## warnings and troubleshooting

* **Deduction Name must be unique:** search the active settings list before creating another item.
* **Deduction Amount is required:** enter a positive numeric amount with no more than two decimal places.
* **Missing from payroll:** review assignment status, pay date or schedule, employee applicability, and recalculation.
* **Deduction continued too long:** review occurrence count or interval end date and the current assignment status.
* **Possible duplicate:** review active and historical employee rows before adding another assignment.
