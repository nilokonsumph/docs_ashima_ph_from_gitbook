# Other earnings

Use Other Earnings for an approved earning that does not belong to basic compensation, allowance, De Minimis, or retroactive pay. Create the reusable definition first, then assign it to the employee.

**Navigation:**\
for setup, go to **Settings → Paycheck → Earnings → Other Earnings**.\
for assignment, go to **Earnings → Other Earnings**, then open the employee.

## when should i use other earnings?

Use this family for a one-time or recurring earning whose business and reporting meaning is not better represented by another Ashima earning family. Do not use it to imitate:

* a salary increase or retroactive-pay result;
* an allowance that requires allowance-specific rate or absence rules; or
* a De Minimis item with a prescribed ceiling and taxable excess.

## before you begin

* confirm the earning name, description, and business purpose;
* confirm the approved amount;
* decide whether it is one-time or recurring;
* for recurring items, decide whether the schedule is an interval or a defined number of occurrences;
* confirm the employee's payroll schedule and allowed pay dates; and
* choose **Taxable** or **Non Taxable** treatment.

## what do the recurrence choices mean?

| choice                 | use it when                                  | required scheduling information                         |
| ---------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| non-recurring          | the earning should be paid once              | an allowed pay date                                     |
| recurring — interval   | the earning should continue between dates    | frequency, payroll schedule, start date, and end date   |
| recurring — occurrence | the earning should stop after a stated count | number of occurrences, payroll schedule, and start date |

The employee assignment form restricts dates to the employee's supported payroll dates. Choose the intended pay date rather than a nearby calendar date.

## how do i create an other-earning definition?

1. Open **Settings → Paycheck → Earnings → Other Earnings**.
2. Select the add action.
3. Enter the earning name, description, and amount.
4. Choose whether the item is recurring.
5. If recurring, select **Interval** or **Occurrence** and complete the displayed schedule fields.
6. If non-recurring, select the intended pay date.
7. Choose the tax rule:
   * **Non Taxable**; or
   * **Taxable**.
8. Review the schedule and tax meaning, then save.

The current assignment screen states that a non-taxable Other Earning is included in the ₱90,000 tax-exemption ceiling handling.

## how do i assign an other earning?

1. Open **Earnings → Other Earnings**.
2. Search for the employee and open the employee detail.
3. Select **Assign Earning**.
4. Select the earning name.
5. Confirm or enter the amount.
6. Select one-time or recurring behavior.
7. Complete the pay date, interval, or occurrence fields displayed by that choice.
8. Confirm the tax rule.
9. Save the assignment.
10. Confirm the new row, then recalculate and review the affected open payroll.

## can an employee have more than one other earning?

Yes, an employee can have different Other Earnings when each record represents a distinct approved item or obligation. Before adding another record with the same earning name, review the employee's active and historical rows. Correct the existing editable assignment when the purpose is to fix an amount or schedule.

## how do i edit, stop, or delete an earning?

Open the employee's Other Earnings detail.

* Use edit to correct an eligible assignment whose business meaning remains the same.
* Use the supported status or schedule controls to stop future recurring use.
* Delete only when the record is eligible and the removal is intended; the page prevents update actions for certain completed records.
* Completed payroll remains historical. Removing an employee assignment does not reverse a closed payroll.

## what happens after saving?

The earning becomes available to payroll according to its pay date or recurring schedule. Review:

* the employee and earning name;
* amount and remaining occurrence or interval;
* pay schedule and selected date;
* tax rule and cumulative ceiling treatment;
* recalculation status of an open payroll; and
* payslip and payroll-register output.

## warnings and troubleshooting

* **Select a date from the allowed pay dates:** use a date from the employee's loaded payroll calendar.
* **No earning name available:** confirm the reusable item exists and is active.
* **Unexpected duplicate:** review active and historical rows before creating another record; use edit for a correction.
* **Missing from payroll:** verify date, schedule, assignment status, employee payroll group, and recalculation.
* **Unexpected taxable amount:** review the tax rule and cumulative ₱90,000 exemption-ceiling handling.
