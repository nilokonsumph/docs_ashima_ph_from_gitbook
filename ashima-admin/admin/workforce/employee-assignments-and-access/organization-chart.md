# Organization chart

use the organization chart to review reporting relationships and direct reports based on the employee reports-to setup.

**Navigation:**\
go to **Workforce → Organization Chart**.

### what does this page display?

the chart shows employees in a reporting hierarchy. Expand a manager to review direct reports and follow the reporting structure.

the hierarchy comes from the employee's reports-to assignment. The chart does not independently create manager visibility or approval authority; those also depend on manager access, privileges, and Workflow settings.

### how do i review the chart?

1. locate the employee or manager in the chart.
2. click **Show direct reports** when it is available.
3. open an employee profile link when more detail is needed.
4. compare unexpected relationships with the employee's employment details.

### how do i print or download the chart?

use **Print**, **Download CSV**, or **Download XLS**. The chart reflects current reports-to assignments, so correct those records before producing the final output.

### why might an employee be missing or under the wrong manager?

check the employee's reports-to assignment, employment status, effective changes, confidential access, and your employee visibility. Correct Employment Details first, then reopen or regenerate the chart.

### does changing the chart change approval routing?

no direct change is made from the chart. Update the employee's reporting relationship in Employment Details. Review Workflow settings separately when the same manager is also expected to approve requests.
