# Confidential employees

use confidential employee settings to restrict sensitive employee and pay information to administrators with confidential access.

> \[!WARNING] changing confidential status affects who may see the employee across supported workforce, payroll, tax, projection, and report pages. Confirm the receiving payroll group and administrator access before saving.

**Navigation:**\
open the employee record, then select **Confidential**.

## what does confidential access control?

confidential access limits the selected employee's records on pages and reports that enforce the confidential-employee rule. It is separate from ordinary page privileges: an administrator needs both access to the module and permission to view confidential employees.

## how do i update confidential status?

1. open the employee record.
2. select **Confidential**.
3. click **Edit** when required.
4. update the confidential setting.
5. select the required payroll-group transfer when the form requests it.
6. review the warning.
7. click **Save**.

## what happens after saving?

administrators without confidential access should no longer see the employee on supported restricted pages, employee lists, and reports. Some operational outputs, such as bank files, may intentionally follow a separate completeness rule. Use the confidential-access review matrix before describing coverage for a specific report.
