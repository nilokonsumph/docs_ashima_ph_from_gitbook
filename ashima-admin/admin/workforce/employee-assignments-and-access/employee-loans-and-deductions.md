# Employee loans and deductions

Use this page as the employee-level route to government loans, third-party loans, insurance, and other deductions. Each family has a separate setup and lifecycle because its balance and reporting meaning differ.

**Navigation:**\
go to **Workforce → Employee assignments and access → Employee loans and deductions**.

### choose the correct employee workflow

| need                         | employee page                          | detailed guide       |
| ---------------------------- | -------------------------------------- | -------------------- |
| SSS or HDMF loan             | **Workforce → Government Loans**       | government loans     |
| company or other lender loan | **Workforce → Third-Party Deductions** | third-party loans    |
| insurance premium deduction  | **Deductions → Insurance**             | insurance deductions |
| another approved deduction   | **Deductions → Other Deductions**      | other deductions     |

### common assignment safeguards

Before assigning any item:

1. confirm the employee and active company item;
2. review existing active and historical assignments;
3. confirm amount, balance, or remittance due;
4. confirm payroll schedule and allowed pay dates;
5. confirm start/end dates, occurrence count, or payment count;
6. save and verify the assignment status; and
7. recalculate and review the affected open payroll.

### how should i stop or correct an assignment?

Use the lifecycle supported by the specific family:

* correct an eligible active record when the same obligation continues;
* pause a loan only when deductions should temporarily stop;
* complete a finite obligation when its balance or payment count is satisfied;
* use an end date or occurrence count for a scheduled stop; and
* delete only an eligible erroneous record after checking open payroll and history.

Removing a current assignment does not rewrite completed payroll. Use the approved payroll-adjustment process for a completed-payroll correction.
