# Employee 201 and documents

use employee 201 to review the employee record set and download workforce information for active, inactive, or end-of-employment employees within your access scope.

**Navigation:**\
go to **Workforce → 201 Files**.

### what does this page display?

the page lists employee identity, personal, contact, government, and employment information. Available values depend on the records maintained for the employee and your privileges.

the 201 list is a consolidated view and export. Update each value on its owning employee page rather than editing a downloaded 201 file as the only correction.

### how do i review an employee 201 record?

1. search for the employee.
2. use the available sort options, such as employee name, gender, marital status, department, employment status, or employment type.
3. review the employee's row and use the related employee pages when more detail is required.

### how do i download employee 201 data?

1. apply the filters you need.
2. select **Excel** or **CSV**.

the export may include employees outside the active roster when the selected option includes inactive or end-of-employment records. Review the scope before sharing the file.

### what should i check before downloading?

* active, inactive, or end-of-employment scope;
* employee and organization filters;
* confidential-employee access;
* completeness of government and contact details; and
* whether the recipient is authorized to receive the exported personal information.

### how is this different from Utilities → Documents?

**Workforce → 201 Files** reviews or exports the employee record dataset. **Utilities → Documents** assigns and prints supported employee documents. A 201 export does not create an assigned document, and removing an assigned document does not remove the underlying employee information.
