# Paycheck surfacing

use **Paycheck Surfacing** to review related employee payroll evidence from one employee-and-date search.

**Navigation:**\
go to **Paycheck → Pay Run → Paycheck Surfacing**.

## how do i use the page?

1. select an employee.
2. enter the from and to dates shown on the page as **Shift from** and **Shift to**.
3. select **search**.
4. open the relevant result family: **payslips**, **Compensation Details**, **Employment Details**, **Timesheets**, **Shift Assigned**, or **Leave Balance**.

## what is this page for?

the page brings related evidence into one review context. Use it to investigate why a payroll value appears as it does before changing a source record or applying a direct Paycheck correction.

## rules and warnings

* a surfaced record remains governed by its source module. Correct it through that module's approved workflow.
* the page's date labels are currently shift-oriented even though the result families include payroll and workforce records; verify the intended date range before searching.
* surfacing is investigation support, not a recalculation, approval, or source-of-truth replacement.
