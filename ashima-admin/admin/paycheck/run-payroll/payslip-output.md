# Payslip output

use a closed payroll masterlist to review, print, or download generated payslips.

**Navigation:**\
go to **Paycheck → Pay Run → Payroll**, find a closed pay run in **pay run history**, then open **View Payroll Masterlist**.

## which controls are available?

the reviewed closed masterlist provided:

* **Download Bank File**;
* **Print Payslip**; and
* **Generate Payslip**.

**Generate Payslip** is unavailable when the required output has already been generated or the current state does not permit regeneration.

## how do i open the print view?

1. select **Print Payslip**.
2. choose **Default**, **By Two / Page**, or **By Four / Page**.
3. wait for the page to finish generating the selected range.
4. select the available range, then use its download or print control.

the current Default view recommends US Legal paper, portrait orientation, shrink-to-fit scaling, and no browser headers or footers.

## what does the payslip show?

the generated output includes company information, employee and payslip identifiers, job and organizational details, pay date and period, basic pay, earnings, deductions, contributions, withholding tax, and net pay. Adjustments and ingestion appear in the payroll section they affect rather than always becoming separate top-level records.

review at least:

* employee identity and payslip number;
* pay date and coverage dates;
* basic pay and regular-hours result;
* adjustment and ingestion lines;
* taxable and non-taxable earnings;
* deductions, contributions, and withholding tax; and
* total earnings, total deductions, and net pay.

## rules and warnings

* a printed or downloaded payslip is an output of the stored closed payroll; do not use it to correct source data.
* **Download Bank File** is a payment-processing output, not proof that the employee received funds.
* protect downloaded payroll and payslip files as confidential employee information.
