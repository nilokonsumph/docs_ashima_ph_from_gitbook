# Individual WTT ingestion

## individual wtt ingestion

use individual WTT ingestion to review and apply a withholding-tax adjustment for one employee in one draft pay run.

> \[!WARNING] the saved amount changes withholding tax in the selected draft pay run. Confirm the employee, pay run, date, and amount before accepting.

**Navigation:**\
open the employee from the supported projection or pay-run adjustment link.

### what does this page display?

the page shows the employee, draft pay run, withholding tax category, current or accepted amount, and recommended amount. The page requires a valid employee and draft pay-run context.

### how do i apply the adjustment?

1. confirm the employee and pay run.
2. review the recommended amount.
3. enter or confirm the withholding-tax amount.
4. click the accept action.
5. review the confirmation.
6. confirm the update.

Do not accept the recommendation when the employee, draft pay run, current tax value, or projection assumptions are no longer current. Correct the source or recalculate first.

### how do i revert it?

use the revert action to restore the previous withholding-tax amount for the employee and draft pay run.

### what happens after acceptance?

the draft pay run uses the accepted withholding-tax adjustment. Recalculate the affected payroll or 13th-month draft when the page instructs you to include the latest update.
