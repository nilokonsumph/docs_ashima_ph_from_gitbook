# Bulk WTT adjustments

use bulk WTT adjustments to apply or revert recommended withholding-tax values for selected employees in one or more open pay runs.

> \[!WARNING] accepting an adjustment overwrites the employee's current withholding tax in the selected open pay run. Review the pay run and recommendation before continuing.

**Navigation:**\
go to **Reports → Tax → Wtt Tool**, then select **Bulk WTT Adjustments**.

## how do i review employees?

1. select one or more open pay runs.
2. apply employee, payroll group, department, status, or amount filters.
3. review the current withholding tax and recommended adjustment per pay.
4. select the employees to update.

## how do i accept recommendations?

1. select the eligible employees.
2. click the accept action.
3. review the confirmation and selected count.
4. confirm the update.

employees with a negative recommended adjustment are excluded from the update in the current bulk flow. Their current withholding tax remains unchanged.

## how do i revert adjustments?

select employees with accepted adjustments, click the revert action, review the confirmation, and continue. Revert restores the previous withholding-tax amount stored for the open pay run.
