# Generate and review projections

use the projection dashboard to recalculate employees, review the calculation breakdown, adjust available assumptions, and download projection data.

**Navigation:**\
go to **Reports → Tax → Wtt Tool**.

## what does the dashboard display?

the table includes employee and payroll-group information, current net taxable, current tax withheld, projected net taxable, projected tax due, projected 13th month, tax gap, tax-gap adjustment, and recommended adjustment.

expand an employee to review lapsed and remaining periods, tax gap, tax withheld, taxable 13th month, plus adjustment, and other calculation details.

## how do i recalculate projections?

1. review the selected year and employee scope.
2. update the 13th-month percentage or plus adjustment when needed.
3. click the recalculation action.
4. monitor the progress display.
5. wait until the batch finishes.
6. review employee exceptions and recommended adjustments.

## how do i adjust the 13th-month assumption?

use the company, payroll-group, bulk employee, or individual option shown on the page. The percentage controls how much basic pay is used as the projected 13th-month amount.

## what downloads are available?

the released page provides Excel, CSV, and import-format exports. The import-format file is intended for a supported adjustment workflow and may require payroll-date completion.
